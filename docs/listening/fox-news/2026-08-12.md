# 2026년 08월 12일 — Republicans Poised for Midterm Gains

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"GOP Sees Path to Historic Midterm Gains as Inflation and Border Security Dominate Voter Priorities"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **poised** | 준비된, 태세를 갖춘 | *The party is poised to reclaim the Senate majority.* |
| **midterm elections** | 중간선거 | *Midterm elections typically test the sitting president's approval rating.* |
| **dominant** | 지배적인, 압도적인 | *Inflation remains the dominant issue among working-class voters.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *With just three months until Election Day, a new Fox News poll shows Republicans with a double-digit enthusiasm gap over Democrats, fueled largely by voter frustration over persistent inflation and border crossings.*

> *Analysts say the political environment mirrors the wave elections of 1994 and 2010, when the party out of power made historic gains in Congress — picking up dozens of House seats in a single night.*

> *The White House is pushing back, arguing that the economy has added over two million jobs this year, and that wages are finally outpacing inflation — a message the administration hopes will resonate with undecided voters in battleground districts.*
