# 2026년 08월 31일 — Fed Signals Rate Cuts Amid Cooling Inflation

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Federal Reserve Chair Signals Potential Rate Cuts as Inflation Eases to Two-Year Low"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **signal** | 신호를 보내다, 암시하다 | *The Fed chair signaled a policy shift at the annual symposium.* |
| **rate cut** | 금리 인하 | *Markets are pricing in three rate cuts before year-end.* |
| **cooling inflation** | 둔화되는 인플레이션 | *Cooling inflation gave the Fed room to pivot.* |
| **pivot** | (정책) 전환 | *Investors anticipated a pivot away from tight monetary policy.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *Monetary policy decisions ripple through the entire economy.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate has held at its highest level in two decades.* |
| **labor market** | 노동 시장 | *A softening labor market reinforced the case for easing.* |
| **easing** | 완화 (금리 인하 방향) | *Financial conditions loosened on expectations of easing.* |
| **Consumer Price Index (CPI)** | 소비자물가지수 | *CPI came in below forecasts for the third straight month.* |
| **yield** | 수익률 | *Treasury yields fell sharply after the Fed chair's remarks.* |
| **dovish** | 완화적인 (금리 인하 선호) | *His tone was more dovish than markets had expected.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell sent markets surging today after hinting that the central bank is ready to begin cutting interest rates as soon as its September meeting.*

> *Inflation, as measured by the Consumer Price Index, dropped to two-point-nine percent last month — the lowest reading in over two years — giving policymakers the confidence they needed to shift course.*

> *Wall Street responded with a broad rally, with the S&P 500 closing up nearly two percent, while Treasury yields tumbled on bets that cheaper borrowing costs are finally on the horizon.*
