# 2026년 10월 07일 — Federal Reserve Holds Rates Amid Economic Uncertainty

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Holds Steady: Interest Rates Unchanged as Officials Monitor Inflation and Jobs Data"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **hold steady** | 현상 유지하다, 안정을 유지하다 | *The central bank decided to hold steady on borrowing costs.* |
| **interest rate** | 금리, 이자율 | *Higher interest rates make mortgages more expensive.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *The Fed's monetary policy aims to balance inflation and employment.* |
| **inflation** | 물가 상승, 인플레이션 | *Inflation has been gradually cooling toward the 2% target.* |
| **unanimous decision** | 만장일치 결정 | *The committee reached a unanimous decision to keep rates unchanged.* |
| **resilient economy** | 탄탄한(회복력 있는) 경제 | *Officials cited a resilient economy as a reason to stay cautious.* |
| **data-dependent** | 데이터 의존적인 | *The Fed's approach is entirely data-dependent.* |
| **labor market** | 노동 시장 | *A tight labor market continues to support consumer spending.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate remains in the 4.75%–5% range.* |
| **press conference** | 기자회견 | *The Fed chair addressed reporters at a press conference Wednesday.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The Federal Reserve voted unanimously Wednesday to hold its benchmark interest rate steady, keeping borrowing costs unchanged as officials wait for more data on inflation and the labor market."*

> *"Fed Chair Jerome Powell told reporters that while inflation has eased considerably, the central bank needs to see sustained progress before considering any further cuts."*

> *"Wall Street responded cautiously to the announcement, with major indexes finishing the day slightly lower as investors recalibrated their expectations for the rest of the year."*
