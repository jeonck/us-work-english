# 2026년 10월 21일 — Federal Reserve Signals Rate Cut Amid Cooling Inflation

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Chair Signals Possible Rate Cut as Inflation Cools to Three-Year Low"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **signal** | 신호를 보내다, 암시하다 | *The Fed chair signaled a possible rate cut next quarter.* |
| **rate cut** | 금리 인하 | *Wall Street rallied on hopes of an imminent rate cut.* |
| **inflation** | 인플레이션, 물가 상승 | *Inflation has cooled significantly over the past six months.* |
| **pivot** | 정책 전환 | *Economists are debating when the Fed will pivot to easing.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *Monetary policy decisions affect mortgage rates and job growth.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate has been held at 4.5% for three meetings.* |
| **dovish** | 완화적인 (금리 인하 선호) | *His remarks were seen as decidedly more dovish than expected.* |
| **hawkish** | 긴축적인 (금리 인상 선호) | *Some committee members remain hawkish, citing wage growth.* |

| **soft landing** | 연착륙 (경기 침체 없는 성장 둔화) | *Policymakers are hoping to engineer a soft landing.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell said Tuesday that the central bank is "carefully monitoring" inflation data and is prepared to adjust its benchmark rate if price pressures continue to ease.*

> *The consumer price index rose just 2.3 percent year-over-year in September — its lowest level in three years — giving policymakers more confidence to consider easing monetary policy before the end of the year.*

> *Stocks surged on Wall Street following the remarks, with the S&P 500 climbing more than one percent as investors priced in a higher probability of a rate cut at the November meeting.*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *price it in* — 시장이 어떤 사건을 이미 주가/채권가격에 반영하다 (e.g., *Markets have already priced in a quarter-point cut.*)
- *ease monetary policy* — 통화 정책을 완화하다, 금리를 낮추다
- *in line with expectations* — 예상에 부합하다 (e.g., *The jobs report came in largely in line with expectations.*)
- *ahead of the curve* — 변화를 미리 예측하고 선제적으로 대응하다
- *tighten the belt* — 허리띠를 졸라매다, 지출을 줄이다
