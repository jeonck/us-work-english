# 2026년 10월 23일 — 2026 Midterm Elections: Senate Control Hangs in the Balance

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"With two weeks to go, both parties are pouring resources into battleground states as control of the Senate remains too close to call."**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **battleground state** | 경합주 (선거에서 결과가 불확실한 주) | *Nevada and Arizona are classic battleground states in every election cycle.* |
| **too close to call** | 결과를 예측하기 어려운 | *The Senate race in Pennsylvania is too close to call with just days remaining.* |
| **pour resources into** | ~에 자원을 쏟아붓다 | *Both parties are pouring resources into key Senate races.* |
| **incumbent** | 현직자, 현직의 | *The incumbent senator faces a tough re-election fight this cycle.* |
| **swing voter** | 부동층 유권자 | *Campaigns are targeting swing voters in suburban districts.* |
| **campaign trail** | 선거 유세 현장 | *The candidate has been on the campaign trail for six consecutive weeks.* |
| **ballot initiative** | 주민 발의 투표 안건 | *A ballot initiative on housing costs is driving voter turnout in California.* |
| **down-ballot** | 하위 선거 (주지사, 시장 등) | *Down-ballot races often go unnoticed but have significant local impact.* |
| **ground game** | 현장 선거 운동 (집집마다 방문, 전화 등) | *The party's strong ground game helped turn out voters in rural counties.* |
| **polling average** | 여론조사 평균 | *According to the latest polling average, the race is within the margin of error.* |

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *neck and neck* — 막상막하의, 아주 접전인 ("The two candidates are neck and neck in the polls.")
- *get out the vote* — 투표 참여를 독려하다 ("Volunteers are calling supporters to get out the vote.")
- *take the lead* — 앞서 나가다, 선두를 차지하다 ("The challenger has taken the lead in the latest surveys.")
