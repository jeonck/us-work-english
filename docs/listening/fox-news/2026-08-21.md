# 2026년 08월 21일 — Federal Reserve Holds Interest Rates Steady

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Keeps Rates Unchanged as Policymakers Weigh Inflation Data Against Slowing Growth"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **hold rates steady** | 금리를 동결하다 | *The Fed voted to hold rates steady at its August meeting.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *Monetary policy decisions affect borrowing costs nationwide.* |
| **inflation data** | 인플레이션 데이터 | *Policymakers are closely watching inflation data before acting.* |
| **slowing growth** | 둔화되는 성장 | *Slowing growth has made the Fed cautious about rate hikes.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate remains at its current level for now.* |
| **dovish stance** | 비둘기파적 입장 (금리 인하 선호) | *Several Fed members took a more dovish stance at the meeting.* |
| **hawkish** | 매파적인 (금리 인상 선호) | *The hawkish wing of the committee pushed for tighter policy.* |
| **unanimous vote** | 만장일치 투표 | *The decision was not unanimous — two members dissented.* |
| **economic outlook** | 경제 전망 | *The Fed revised its economic outlook downward for 2026.* |
| **labor market** | 노동 시장 | *A resilient labor market complicates the case for rate cuts.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The Federal Reserve announced today that it will hold its benchmark interest rate steady, citing ongoing uncertainty about both inflation and economic growth."*

> *"Fed Chair Jerome Powell told reporters that while inflation has moderated, policymakers want to see more sustained progress before considering any rate cuts."*

> *"Markets responded cautiously to the announcement, with major indices dipping slightly as investors processed the central bank's latest economic projections."*

2. What two economic factors is the Fed weighing before making future rate decisions?
3. How did financial markets react to the Fed's announcement?

2. The Fed is weighing ongoing inflation data against slowing economic growth.

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *weigh A against B* — A와 B를 저울질하다, 비교 검토하다
- *cite + 이유* — ~을 이유로 들다 (공식 발표에서 자주 쓰임)
- *moderate* (동사) — (인플레이션 등이) 완화되다, 누그러지다
