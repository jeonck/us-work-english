# 2026년 09월 26일 — Federal Reserve Holds Rates as Inflation Outlook Shifts

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Keeps Benchmark Rate Steady, Signals Cautious Path Forward as Core Inflation Edges Lower"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The Fed left the benchmark rate unchanged at its September meeting.* |
| **hold steady** | 유지하다, 변동 없이 유지하다 | *Policymakers voted to hold steady despite pressure from markets.* |
| **core inflation** | 근원 물가상승률 (식품·에너지 제외) | *Core inflation edged lower for the third consecutive month.* |
| **cautious path forward** | 신중한 앞으로의 행보 | *The Fed chair outlined a cautious path forward amid global uncertainty.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *Tight monetary policy has helped cool consumer spending.* |
| **hawkish / dovish** | 매파적(긴축 선호) / 비둘기파적(완화 선호) | *Several committee members shifted to a more dovish stance this quarter.* |
| **unanimous vote** | 만장일치 표결 | *The decision was passed by a unanimous vote of the committee.* |
| **data-dependent** | 데이터에 따라 결정하는 | *Officials stressed that future moves remain data-dependent.* |
| **treasury yield** | 국채 수익률 | *Ten-year treasury yields dipped following the announcement.* |
| **rate cut outlook** | 금리 인하 전망 | *Investors repriced the rate cut outlook after the press conference.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *The Federal Reserve voted unanimously today to hold its benchmark interest rate steady, signaling that policymakers want more evidence that inflation is truly on a sustained downward path before making any further adjustments.*

> *Fed Chair Jerome Powell told reporters at a press conference that the committee remains data-dependent, and that the pace of future rate decisions will hinge on incoming economic data, including jobs numbers and consumer price reports.*

> *Markets responded cautiously to the announcement — stocks edged slightly higher while treasury yields dipped, as investors recalibrated their expectations for when the first rate cut of the cycle might arrive.*

## 이해도 확인

1. What did the Federal Reserve decide at its September meeting?
2. What condition did Fed officials say must be met before they consider changing rates?
3. How did financial markets react to the Fed's announcement?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. The Fed voted unanimously to hold its benchmark interest rate steady, making no change.
2. Officials said they need more evidence that inflation is on a sustained downward path before making any further adjustments — they emphasized that all future decisions remain data-dependent.
3. Markets reacted cautiously but positively: stocks edged slightly higher and treasury yields dipped as investors adjusted their expectations for future rate cuts.

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *hinge on* — ~에 달려 있다 (예: *Everything hinges on next month's jobs report.*)
- *edge lower / edge higher* — 소폭 하락하다 / 소폭 상승하다 (예: *Core inflation edged lower for the second straight month.*)
- *reprice expectations* — 기대치를 재조정하다 (예: *Traders repriced their rate-cut expectations after the speech.*)
- *in line with forecasts* — 예측과 일치하여 (예: *The decision was in line with most Wall Street forecasts.*)
- *unanimous vote* — 만장일치 표결 (예: *The committee passed the measure by unanimous vote.*)
