# 2026년 09월 02일 — Federal Reserve Holds Interest Rates Steady

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Holds Rates Steady as Officials Weigh Inflation Progress Against Economic Slowdown Risks"**

---

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **hold rates steady** | 금리를 동결하다 | *The Fed voted to hold rates steady for the third consecutive meeting.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *Monetary policy decisions affect borrowing costs across the economy.* |
| **inflation** | 인플레이션, 물가 상승 | *Inflation has cooled significantly from its peak two years ago.* |

| **Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC)** | 연방공개시장위원회 | *The FOMC meets eight times a year to review monetary policy.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *The Federal Reserve voted unanimously today to hold its benchmark interest rate steady, keeping borrowing costs at their current level as policymakers assess the path of inflation.*

> *Fed Chair Jerome Powell told reporters that while inflation has made meaningful progress toward the central bank's two-percent target, officials want to see more data before making any moves.*

> *Markets rallied on the news, with investors interpreting the Fed's cautious tone as a signal that rate cuts could be on the table later this year, provided economic conditions continue to improve.*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *on the table* — 논의 대상에 오른, 검토 중인 (e.g., *Rate cuts are on the table for next quarter.*)
- *provided that* — ~라는 조건 하에, ~한다면 (e.g., *We'll proceed, provided that the data supports it.*)
- *benchmark rate* — 기준 금리 (e.g., *The benchmark rate influences all other borrowing costs.*)
- *policymakers* — 정책 입안자들 (e.g., *Policymakers are divided on the next course of action.*)
- *unanimously* — 만장일치로 (e.g., *The committee voted unanimously to hold rates.*)
