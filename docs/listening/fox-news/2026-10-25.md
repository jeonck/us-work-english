## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *brace for* — ~에 대비하다 (e.g., *We should brace for a tight race on election night.*)
- *pour resources into* — ~에 자원을 쏟아붓다 (e.g., *Both campaigns are pouring resources into get-out-the-vote efforts.*)
- *heat up* — 열기가 달아오르다, 격렬해지다 (e.g., *The political debate is heating up as November approaches.*)
