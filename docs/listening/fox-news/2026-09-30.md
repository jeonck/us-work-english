# 2026년 09월 30일 — Fed Holds Rates Steady Amid Economic Uncertainty

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Federal Reserve Holds Interest Rates Steady, Signals Caution as Inflation Remains Stubborn"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **hold steady** | 현상 유지하다, 안정적으로 유지하다 | *The Fed voted to hold steady on interest rates for the third consecutive meeting.* |
| **interest rate** | 금리, 이자율 | *Rising interest rates have made it harder for families to afford mortgages.* |
| **inflation** | 인플레이션, 물가 상승 | *Stubborn inflation continues to weigh on consumer spending.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *The central bank's monetary policy decisions affect borrowing costs nationwide.* |
| **consecutive** | 연속적인 | *This is the Fed's third consecutive meeting with no rate change.* |
| **stubborn** | 완강한, 잘 꺾이지 않는 | *Stubborn core inflation has kept policymakers on alert.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate currently stands at 4.25 percent.* |
| **cautious stance** | 신중한 입장 | *Fed Chair adopted a cautious stance, warning against premature rate cuts.* |
| **tighten** | 긴축하다 | *The Fed tightened policy aggressively over the past two years.* |
| **outlook** | 전망, 예측 | *The economic outlook remains uncertain heading into the fourth quarter.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The Federal Reserve wrapped up its two-day policy meeting today, voting unanimously to hold its benchmark interest rate steady — leaving borrowing costs unchanged for the third meeting in a row."*

> *"Fed Chair Jerome Powell told reporters the central bank remains data-dependent, stressing that officials need to see more progress on inflation before considering any cuts."*

> *"Markets reacted cautiously to the announcement, with the Dow Jones Industrial Average closing down slightly as investors digested the Fed's wait-and-see approach."*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *data-dependent* — 데이터에 근거하여 결정하는 (지표 의존적)
- *wait-and-see approach* — 관망 전략 (상황을 지켜보며 결정을 미루는 방식)
- *borrowing costs* — 대출 비용 (금리 수준에 따른 차입 비용)
- *rate cut* — 금리 인하
- *central bank* — 중앙은행
