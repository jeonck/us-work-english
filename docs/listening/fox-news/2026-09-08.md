## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *The Federal Reserve announced today it will keep its benchmark interest rate unchanged, citing ongoing concerns about inflation that remains above its two-percent target.*

> *Fed Chair stated that while the labor market has shown remarkable resilience, policymakers are not yet confident that inflation is sustainably moving toward their goal.*

> *Wall Street responded with mixed signals, as investors recalibrated their expectations for rate cuts later this year, with some now pushing their forecasts into early next year.*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *tighten monetary policy* — 통화 정책을 긴축하다 (금리를 올리거나 유지하여 경기를 억제하는 것)
- *dovish outlook* — 비둘기파적 전망 (금리 인하를 선호하는 완화적 입장)
- *front-load rate hikes* — 금리 인상을 조기에 집중시키다 (초반에 강하게 올리는 전략)
- *soft landing* — 연착륙 (경기 침체 없이 인플레이션을 잡는 시나리오)
- *price stability* — 물가 안정 (중앙은행의 핵심 정책 목표 중 하나)
