# 2026년 08월 30일 — U.S.-South Korea Trade Deal Amid China Tensions

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"White House Finalizes Landmark Trade Agreement With South Korea as Beijing Watches Closely"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **landmark** | 획기적인, 역사적인 | *This is a landmark agreement that will reshape Pacific trade.* |
| **bilateral** | 양자 간의 (두 나라 사이의) | *The bilateral talks lasted three days before a deal was reached.* |
| **tariff** | 관세 | *New tariffs on Chinese goods came into effect this month.* |
| **trade deficit** | 무역 적자 | *The administration aims to reduce the trade deficit with key partners.* |
| **supply chain** | 공급망 | *Diversifying the supply chain away from China is a top priority.* |
| **leverage** | 영향력, 지렛대 | *The U.S. used its military presence as leverage in negotiations.* |
| **ratify** | 비준하다 (공식 승인하다) | *Both legislatures must ratify the deal before it takes effect.* |
| **strategic ally** | 전략적 동맹국 | *South Korea remains one of America's most important strategic allies.* |
| **concession** | 양보, 타협안 | *Both sides made concessions on agricultural and tech exports.* |
| **geopolitical** | 지정학적인 | *The agreement carries significant geopolitical weight in the region.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *The White House announced a landmark bilateral trade agreement with South Korea on Friday, a deal officials say will cut tariffs, strengthen supply chains, and send a clear message to Beijing.*

> *Administration officials called the agreement a win for American manufacturers and a critical step in reducing dependence on Chinese-made components, especially in semiconductors and electric vehicle batteries.*

> *South Korean officials welcomed the deal, saying it reinforces the alliance and opens new opportunities for Korean tech firms looking to expand into the American market.*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *send a clear message* — 분명한 메시지를 보내다 (외교·협상에서 자주 씀)
- *take effect* — 발효되다, 효력이 생기다
- *a win for* — ~에게 유리한 결과, 승리
- *look to expand into* — ~로 진출하려고 하다
- *reinforce the alliance* — 동맹을 강화하다
