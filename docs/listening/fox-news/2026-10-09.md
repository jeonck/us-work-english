# 2026년 10월 09일 — Fed Rate Decision Sparks Market Debate

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Federal Reserve Holds Rates Steady Amid Inflation Concerns, Wall Street Reacts"**

> *"The Federal Reserve voted unanimously today to hold its benchmark interest rate steady, citing persistent inflation pressures and a resilient labor market."*

> *"Fed Chair Jerome Powell emphasized that the central bank remains data-dependent, meaning future rate decisions will hinge on incoming economic reports."*

> *"Wall Street responded with mixed signals — the Dow Jones edged higher while the tech-heavy Nasdaq slipped as investors recalibrated their expectations for rate cuts next year."*

## 이해도 확인

1. What did the Federal Reserve decide about interest rates today?
2. What does "data-dependent" mean in this context?
3. How did the stock market respond to the announcement?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. The Federal Reserve voted to hold its benchmark interest rate steady (unchanged).
2. "Data-dependent" means that future rate decisions will be based on incoming economic data and reports, rather than a fixed schedule.
3. The market reacted with mixed signals — the Dow Jones edged higher while the Nasdaq slipped, as investors adjusted their expectations for future rate cuts.

</details>

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *on edge* — 불안해하는, 긴장한 (e.g., *Investors are on edge ahead of the announcement.*)
- *hinge on* — ~에 달려 있다 (e.g., *Everything hinges on the jobs report.*)
- *recalibrate expectations* — 기대치를 재조정하다 (e.g., *Traders recalibrated their expectations after the speech.*)
- *edge higher / edge lower* — 소폭 상승/하락하다 (e.g., *The index edged higher by 0.3%.*)
- *resilient labor market* — 강한 노동 시장 (e.g., *A resilient labor market complicates the Fed's decisions.*)
