# 2026년 10월 05일 — Federal Reserve Holds Rates as Inflation Stays Stubborn

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Stands Pat on Interest Rates, Warning Inflation Has Yet to Be Fully Conquered"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **stand pat** | 현 상태를 유지하다 | *The Fed chose to stand pat rather than cut rates this quarter.* |
| **inflation** | 물가 상승, 인플레이션 | *Inflation remains above the Fed's two-percent target.* |
| **interest rate** | 금리, 이자율 | *Higher interest rates make borrowing more expensive for consumers.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *Monetary policy decisions affect everything from mortgages to car loans.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The Fed kept its benchmark rate unchanged at five-and-a-half percent.* |
| **tighten (policy)** | (정책을) 긴축하다 | *The central bank has been tightening policy for over two years.* |
| **stubborn** | 완강한, 좀처럼 낮아지지 않는 | *Stubborn core inflation is making it hard to justify rate cuts.* |
| **pivot** | 방향 전환 | *Wall Street has been waiting for the Fed to pivot toward rate cuts.* |
| **consumer spending** | 소비자 지출 | *Strong consumer spending is keeping price pressures elevated.* |
| **unanimous decision** | 만장일치 결정 | *The vote to hold rates steady was a unanimous decision by the committee.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *The Federal Reserve voted unanimously today to hold its benchmark interest rate steady, signaling that the central bank is not yet convinced that inflation has been brought under control.*

> *Fed Chair Jerome Powell told reporters that while progress has been made, core inflation remains stubbornly above the two-percent target, and the committee needs to see more data before it can consider cutting rates.*

> *Markets had widely expected the Fed to stand pat, but some investors were hoping for a signal that a rate cut could come before the end of the year — a signal Powell notably declined to give.*
