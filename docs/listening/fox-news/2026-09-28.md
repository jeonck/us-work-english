# 2026년 09월 28일 — Congress Races to Avoid Government Shutdown

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Congress scrambles to pass a continuing resolution as the September 30 funding deadline looms, with both chambers divided over spending cuts and border security provisions."**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **government shutdown** | 정부 업무 정지 | *A government shutdown would furlough hundreds of thousands of federal workers.* |
| **continuing resolution (CR)** | 임시 예산안 | *Congress passed a continuing resolution to keep the government funded for 45 days.* |
| **funding deadline** | 예산 마감 기한 | *Lawmakers worked through the weekend ahead of the funding deadline.* |
| **scramble** | 허둥지둥 서두르다 | *Both parties scrambled to reach a deal before midnight.* |
| **looms** | (위협적으로) 다가오다 | *The possibility of a shutdown looms large as talks stall.* |
| **provisions** | 조항, 규정 | *Democrats objected to border security provisions in the spending bill.* |
| **bipartisan** | 초당파적인 | *A bipartisan group of senators pushed for a clean spending bill.* |
| **fiscal year** | 회계연도 | *The federal government's fiscal year ends on September 30.* |
| **furlough** | 일시 해고, 강제 휴직 | *Non-essential federal employees would be furloughed during a shutdown.* |
| **stall** | 교착 상태에 빠지다 | *Negotiations stalled over disagreements on discretionary spending levels.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"With just two days until the federal government runs out of funding, lawmakers on Capitol Hill are working around the clock to hammer out a deal."*

> *"Republican leadership is pushing for deep cuts to non-defense discretionary spending, while Democrats insist that any short-term bill be free of policy riders."*

> *"If no agreement is reached by midnight on September 30th, federal agencies would begin shutdown procedures, affecting millions of Americans who rely on government services."*

## 이해도 확인

1. What is the specific deadline Congress must meet to avoid a government shutdown?
2. What are the two main points of disagreement between Republicans and Democrats?
3. Who would be most directly affected if the government shuts down?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. The deadline is midnight on September 30th, the last day of the federal fiscal year.
2. Republicans want deep cuts to non-defense discretionary spending, while Democrats oppose policy riders (such as border security provisions) attached to the spending bill.
3. Federal employees would be furloughed (temporarily laid off), and millions of Americans who rely on government services would be directly affected.

</details>

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *work around the clock* — 밤낮없이 일하다 ("The team worked around the clock to meet the deadline.")
- *hammer out a deal* — 협상 끝에 합의에 이르다 ("After hours of talks, they finally hammered out a compromise.")
- *policy rider* — (법안에 붙이는) 부가 조항 ("The spending bill was held up by a controversial policy rider.")
- *clean bill* — 부가 조항 없는 순수 법안 ("Opposition lawmakers demanded a clean bill with no policy attachments.")
- *run out of funding* — 예산이 바닥나다 ("The agency will run out of funding if Congress doesn't act.")
