# 2026년 09월 29일 — Federal Reserve Holds Rates Amid Inflation Concerns

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Holds Steady: Interest Rates Unchanged as Policymakers Monitor Inflation Trajectory"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **hold steady** | 유지하다, 안정적으로 유지하다 | *The Fed decided to hold steady on interest rates this quarter.* |
| **interest rate** | 금리 | *A rise in interest rates makes borrowing more expensive.* |
| **inflation trajectory** | 물가 상승 궤적 | *Economists are watching the inflation trajectory closely.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *The central bank's monetary policy affects every consumer.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate was left unchanged at the September meeting.* |
| **tighten** | 긴축하다 | *The Fed may tighten policy further if prices keep rising.* |
| **dovish** | 비둘기파적인 (완화적) | *Several board members expressed a more dovish outlook.* |
| **hawkish** | 매파적인 (긴축적) | *The hawkish wing of the committee pushed for another rate hike.* |
| **pivot** | 방향 전환 | *Markets are betting on a policy pivot early next year.* |
| **yield curve** | 수익률 곡선 | *An inverted yield curve often signals economic slowdown.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The Federal Reserve voted unanimously today to keep its benchmark interest rate unchanged, as officials said they need more data before making their next move."*

> *"Fed Chair Jerome Powell told reporters the central bank remains committed to bringing inflation back down to its two-percent target, while also keeping a close eye on the labor market."*

> *"Investors had widely anticipated the pause, but Wall Street is now turning its attention to the December meeting, where another hold — or a potential rate cut — could be on the table."*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *on hold* — 보류 중인, 중단된 상태 (e.g., "Rate cuts are on hold until inflation cools.")
- *tighten the belt* — 허리띠를 졸라매다, 긴축하다 (e.g., "Consumers are tightening their belts amid high prices.")
- *ahead of schedule* — 예정보다 앞서 (e.g., "Inflation came down ahead of schedule.")
