# 2026년 08월 29일 — Fed Signals Potential Rate Cut as Economy Shows Signs of Cooling

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Federal Reserve Chair signals openness to interest rate cuts as inflation eases and labor market softens, raising hopes for economic relief heading into fall."**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **signal** | (신호를) 보내다, 암시하다 | *The Fed chair signaled a possible shift in monetary policy.* |
| **rate cut** | 금리 인하 | *Markets rallied on news of a potential rate cut.* |
| **inflation** | 인플레이션, 물가 상승 | *Inflation has eased from its peak of over 4 percent.* |
| **labor market** | 노동 시장 | *A softening labor market typically precedes a rate cut.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *The Fed adjusts monetary policy to keep prices stable.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate has remained unchanged for six months.* |
| **dovish** | 완화적인, 비둘기파적인 | *His remarks were seen as dovish by Wall Street analysts.* |
| **easing** | 완화 (금리·긴축 등의 완화) | *Investors are pricing in two rounds of easing before year-end.* |
| **consumer spending** | 소비자 지출 | *Weaker consumer spending is a key sign of cooling demand.* |
| **yield** | 수익률, 채권 금리 | *Treasury yields dropped sharply after the Fed's announcement.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell hinted Friday that the central bank may be ready to pivot, telling reporters that inflation is moving sustainably toward the two-percent target.*

> *Stocks surged and Treasury yields tumbled as investors bet the Fed will cut its benchmark rate at the September meeting — potentially for the first time in over two years.*

> *But not everyone is convinced. Some economists warn that cutting too soon could reignite inflation, while others argue that a slowing labor market leaves the Fed with little choice.*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *pivot* — (정책 방향을) 전환하다 ("The Fed is expected to pivot toward easing.")
- *price in* — (시장이) 이미 반영하다 ("Markets have already priced in two rate cuts.")
- *reignite* — 다시 불붙이다, 재점화하다 ("Cutting too early could reignite inflation.")
