# 2026년 09월 27일 — Federal Reserve Holds Interest Rates Steady Amid Inflation Concerns

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Holds Rates Steady, Signals Cautious Approach as Inflation Remains Above Target"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **hold steady** | 변동 없이 유지하다 | *The Fed voted to hold rates steady for the third consecutive meeting.* |
| **inflation** | 인플레이션, 물가 상승 | *Inflation remains stubbornly above the Fed's 2% target.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate currently sits at 4.5%.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *The committee reviewed its monetary policy stance carefully.* |
| **cautious approach** | 신중한 접근 방식 | *Officials are taking a cautious approach before cutting rates.* |
| **pivot** | 방향 전환 | *Wall Street is eagerly awaiting a Fed pivot toward rate cuts.* |
| **labor market** | 노동 시장 | *A robust labor market has complicated the Fed's decision-making.* |
| **consumer spending** | 소비자 지출 | *Consumer spending stayed resilient despite higher borrowing costs.* |
| **press conference** | 기자 회견 | *Fed Chair held a press conference following the rate decision.* |
| **economic outlook** | 경제 전망 | *The Fed revised its economic outlook for the rest of the year.* |
