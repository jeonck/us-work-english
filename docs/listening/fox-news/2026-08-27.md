## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Republicans poised to expand Senate majority as key battleground races tighten ahead of November midterms."**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **poised to** | ~할 준비가 된, ~할 태세인 | *The party is poised to flip several swing-state seats.* |
| **battleground (state/race)** | 경합지 (주 / 선거) | *Arizona remains a critical battleground state heading into November.* |
| **tighten** | (격차가) 좁혀지다, 팽팽해지다 | *The polls have tightened significantly over the past month.* |
| **majority** | (의회) 다수당, 과반수 | *Republicans need two more seats to expand their Senate majority.* |
| **incumbent** | 현직 의원, 현직자 | *The incumbent senator is trailing by three points in the latest poll.* |
| **canvassing** | 유권자 호별 방문 운동 | *Volunteers spent the weekend canvassing neighborhoods in swing districts.* |

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *flip a seat* — (반대 당이) 의석을 빼앗다 ("Can Democrats flip a red seat in Texas?")
- *hold the line* — 현 상태를 유지하다, 선을 지키다 ("They're working hard to hold the line in key districts.")
- *double down on* — ~에 더욱 집중하다, 배로 베팅하다 ("The campaign doubled down on its economic message.")
- *heading into the final stretch* — 막판에 접어들며 ("Momentum is shifting heading into the final stretch of the campaign.")
- *drive enthusiasm* — 열기를 불러일으키다 ("The debate seemed to drive enthusiasm among younger voters.")
