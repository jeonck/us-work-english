## 이해도 확인

1. What is the current political situation with the midterm elections?
2. What issues are most important to voters according to the broadcast?
3. What does "razor-thin margin" mean, and why is it significant in this context?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. Both parties are making a final push with three weeks until Election Day. Republicans are trying to expand their Senate majority while Democrats are fighting to hold key seats. Polls show very close races in battleground states.
2. Voters say the economy, immigration, and healthcare are their top concerns heading into the midterm elections.
3. "Razor-thin margin" means an extremely small difference between candidates. It is significant because it means the election outcome is uncertain and even a small change in voter turnout could determine the winner.

</details>

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *hang in the balance* — 결과가 불확실한 상태이다 (운명이 갈림길에 있다)
- *pour resources into* — ~에 자원을 집중 투입하다
- *hold onto* — ~을 유지하다, 놓지 않다
- *battle it out* — 끝까지 싸우다, 접전을 벌이다
- *make a final push* — 마지막 총력전을 펼치다
