> **"U.S. Trade Deficit Falls to a Three-Year Low as Sweeping Tariff Measures Begin to Reshape Global Supply Chains"**

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *pay off* — (노력·투자가) 성과를 내다, 결실을 맺다 ("The strategy is finally paying off.")
- *push back* — 반발하다, 저항하다 ("Other nations are pushing back against the new rules.")
- *rattle global markets* — 세계 시장을 흔들다 ("Unexpected news can rattle global markets overnight.")
