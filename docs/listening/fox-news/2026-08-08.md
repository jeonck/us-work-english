# 2026년 08월 08일 — Congress Debates Debt Ceiling as National Debt Hits Record $37 Trillion

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Congress faces a critical deadline to raise the debt ceiling as the national debt surpasses $37 trillion, sparking fierce debate over government spending and fiscal responsibility."**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **debt ceiling** | 부채 한도 | *Congress must vote to raise the debt ceiling before the government runs out of money.* |
| **fiscal responsibility** | 재정 책임 | *Lawmakers from both parties claimed to support fiscal responsibility.* |
| **bipartisan** | 초당파적인, 양당 합의의 | *The bill passed with bipartisan support from both Republicans and Democrats.* |
| **default** | 채무 불이행 | *Failing to raise the debt ceiling could cause the U.S. to default on its obligations.* |
| **deficit spending** | 적자 지출 | *Critics argue that deficit spending has contributed to the record national debt.* |
| **appropriations** | 예산 배정 | *Congress must pass appropriations bills to fund government programs.* |
| **contingency plan** | 비상 계획 | *The Treasury Department outlined a contingency plan in case talks collapsed.* |
| **austerity measures** | 긴축 조치 | *Some lawmakers are pushing for austerity measures to bring down the deficit.* |
| **spending cuts** | 지출 삭감 | *Republicans are demanding spending cuts in exchange for raising the debt ceiling.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

> *"Republicans on Capitol Hill say they will not support raising the debt ceiling without significant spending cuts attached, while the White House insists the government must meet its existing obligations without conditions."*

> *"With the national debt now exceeding thirty-seven trillion dollars, economists on both sides of the aisle warn that the longer Congress delays, the greater the risk to financial markets and the broader American economy."*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *high-stakes showdown* — 결과가 중대한 대결, 긴박한 맞대결
- *run out of money* — 자금이 바닥나다
- *meet its existing obligations* — 기존 의무를 이행하다
- *both sides of the aisle* — 여야 양쪽 (의회 복도 양쪽에 앉는 데서 유래)
- *without conditions* — 조건 없이
