# 2026년 09월 16일 — Federal Reserve Signals Rate Cut as Inflation Cools

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Chair Signals September Rate Cut as Inflation Finally Retreats to Near Target"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **rate cut** | 금리 인하 | *The Fed's rate cut boosted stock markets overnight.* |
| **inflation** | 인플레이션, 물가 상승 | *Inflation has cooled significantly over the past six months.* |
| **retreat** | 후퇴하다, (수치가) 떨어지다 | *Consumer prices retreated to a two-year low.* |
| **pivot** | 방향 전환 | *Investors are betting on a Fed policy pivot this fall.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준금리 | *The benchmark rate has held steady at 5.25% for over a year.* |
| **labor market** | 노동 시장 | *A cooling labor market gave the Fed room to act.* |
| **consumer confidence** | 소비자 신뢰 지수 | *Consumer confidence dipped slightly amid global uncertainty.* |
| **fiscal policy** | 재정 정책 | *Analysts say fiscal policy must complement the Fed's move.* |
| **soft landing** | 연착륙 (경기 침체 없이 인플레 진정) | *The Fed is hoping to achieve a soft landing for the economy.* |
| **yield** | 수익률 | *Treasury yields fell sharply after the announcement.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The Federal Reserve is widely expected to cut interest rates at its upcoming September meeting, as inflation data continues to show a steady decline toward the central bank's two-percent target."*

> *"Fed Chair Jerome Powell said the time has come to begin adjusting policy, signaling that years of aggressive rate hikes may finally be coming to an end — though he stressed the Fed will move carefully and gradually."*

> *"Markets reacted swiftly, with the Dow Jones Industrial Average surging more than four hundred points and the ten-year Treasury yield dropping to its lowest level in eighteen months."*

## 이해도 확인

1. Why is the Federal Reserve expected to cut interest rates?
2. What did Fed Chair Powell emphasize about the pace of future rate changes?
3. How did financial markets respond to the Fed's announcement?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. Inflation data has been steadily declining toward the Fed's two-percent target, giving the central bank confidence to begin easing policy.
2. Powell stressed that the Fed will move carefully and gradually, even though he signaled that the era of aggressive rate hikes is ending.
3. Markets reacted positively — the Dow Jones surged more than 400 points and the 10-year Treasury yield fell to an 18-month low.

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *hold steady* — (금리·수치가) 변동 없이 유지되다 ("Rates held steady for twelve consecutive months.")
- *ease monetary policy* — 통화 정책을 완화하다 ("The Fed is preparing to ease monetary policy for the first time in four years.")
- *tighten the belt* — 허리띠를 졸라매다, 긴축하다 ("Consumers are tightening their belts as borrowing costs remain high.")
