# 2026년 09월 13일 — Midterm Election Economy: Voters Focus on Inflation and Jobs

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"With midterms weeks away, polls show economy remains voters' top concern as inflation lingers and job market cools"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **midterm elections** | 중간선거 | *Midterm elections are held two years into a presidential term.* |
| **inflation** | 물가 상승, 인플레이션 | *Persistent inflation has eroded consumers' purchasing power.* |
| **linger** | 지속되다, 오래 남다 | *High prices continue to linger despite rate hikes.* |
| **purchasing power** | 구매력 | *Rising costs have cut into the purchasing power of middle-class families.* |
| **swing district** | 경합 지역구 | *Candidates in swing districts are focusing on pocketbook issues.* |
| **pocketbook issue** | 경제적 생활 문제 | *Jobs and wages are classic pocketbook issues in any election.* |
| **cool** (경제 맥락) | 둔화되다, 식다 | *Analysts say the job market is beginning to cool after years of growth.* |
| **incumbent** | 현직자 | *The incumbent senator faces a tough re-election battle over the economy.* |
| **toss-up race** | 초접전 선거 | *Several Senate seats are now rated toss-up races by forecasters.* |
| **fiscal policy** | 재정 정책 | *Voters are divided over the right fiscal policy to tackle the deficit.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"With November's midterm elections now just weeks away, a new national poll shows that the economy remains the number-one issue for American voters — and both parties are scrambling to own the message."*

> *"Prices at the grocery store and gas pump are still higher than they were three years ago, and that lingering inflation is fueling frustration among working families across the country."*

> *"Forecasters say nearly two dozen House seats and at least five Senate seats are currently rated as toss-up races, meaning control of Congress could hinge on which party voters trust more to handle the economy."*

## 이해도 확인

1. What is the number-one concern for American voters according to the poll?
2. Why are working families frustrated?
3. What makes a race a "toss-up"?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. The economy is the top concern for voters heading into the midterm elections.
2. Prices at grocery stores and gas pumps remain higher than three years ago, meaning lingering inflation is hitting household budgets.
3. A race is a toss-up when it is so close that either candidate could win, meaning the outcome is uncertain.

</details>
