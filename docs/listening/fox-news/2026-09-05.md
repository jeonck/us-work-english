# 2026년 09월 05일 — Fed Rate Decision Sparks Wall Street Rally

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *The Federal Reserve cut its benchmark interest rate by a quarter point today, the third reduction this year, as policymakers expressed growing confidence that inflation is on a sustained path toward their two-percent target.*

> *Wall Street surged on the news, with the Dow Jones Industrial Average climbing more than four hundred points, while the S&P 500 hit a fresh record high on expectations of further easing ahead.*

> *Fed Chair Jerome Powell told reporters that the committee remains data-dependent and will not hesitate to pause if inflation shows signs of re-accelerating, but added that the overall economic outlook remains, quote, "solid."*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *ease monetary policy* — 통화 정책을 완화하다
- *tighten credit conditions* — 신용 조건을 조이다
- *front-load rate cuts* — 초반에 금리 인하를 집중시키다
- *soft landing* — 경기 연착륙 (경기 침체 없이 인플레이션을 잡는 것)
- *hawkish stance* — 매파적 입장 (금리 인상 선호)
- *on hold* — (정책을) 동결 상태로 두다
