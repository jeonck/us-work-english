# 2026년 09월 11일 — Federal Reserve Holds Rates Amid Mixed Economic Signals

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Keeps Interest Rates Steady as Inflation Cools but Labor Market Shows Cracks"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **hold steady** | 현상 유지하다, 안정을 지키다 | *The Fed decided to hold rates steady at its September meeting.* |
| **inflation** | 인플레이션, 물가 상승 | *Inflation cooled to 2.4% on an annual basis.* |
| **labor market** | 노동 시장 | *Cracks in the labor market have economists on edge.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate remains in the 4.25–4.50% range.* |
| **dovish** | (통화정책이) 완화적인, 비둘기파적 | *Several Fed officials signaled a more dovish stance going forward.* |
| **pivot** | (정책) 전환 | *Markets are pricing in a pivot to rate cuts by year-end.* |
| **gross domestic product (GDP)** | 국내총생산 | *GDP growth slowed to 1.8% in the second quarter.* |
| **stagflation** | 스태그플레이션 (경기침체+물가상승) | *Analysts warned of stagflation risk if growth keeps slowing.* |
| **yield curve** | 수익률 곡선 | *An inverted yield curve has historically signaled recessions.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *Monetary policy tightening over the past two years weighed on housing.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The Federal Reserve held its benchmark interest rate steady on Wednesday, citing a gradual easing in inflation while expressing concern over a softening labor market."*

> *"Fed Chair Jerome Powell said policymakers remain data-dependent, adding that the central bank is prepared to cut rates if the economy weakens further than expected."*

> *"Wall Street responded positively to the announcement, with the S&P 500 climbing more than one percent as investors interpreted the statement as a signal of an imminent rate cut."*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *on the fence* — 결정을 못 내리고 있는 (The Fed is still on the fence about the timing of cuts.)
- *tighten the belt* — 허리띠를 졸라매다, 긴축하다 (Consumers are tightening their belts amid high borrowing costs.)
- *ahead of the curve* — 시대를 앞서 나가다, 선제적으로 대응하다 (The Fed wants to act ahead of the curve to prevent a recession.)
