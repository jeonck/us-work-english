# 2026년 10월 16일 — Midterm Election Countdown

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"With three weeks to go, Republicans look to hold the Senate as Democrats surge in key battleground states."**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **midterm election** | 중간선거 | *The midterm election will determine control of Congress.* |
| **battleground state** | 경합 주 (결과가 불확실한 주) | *Ohio has been a battleground state for decades.* |
| **surge** | 급등하다, 급증 | *Support for the candidate surged after the debate.* |
| **hold the Senate** | 상원을 유지하다 | *Republicans are fighting hard to hold the Senate.* |
| **toss-up** | 오차범위 내 / 결과 예측 불가 | *Three Senate races are rated as toss-ups heading into November.* |
| **turnout** | 투표율 | *High voter turnout usually benefits Democrats in urban areas.* |
| **incumbent** | 현직자, 현직 의원 | *The incumbent senator faces a tough re-election challenge.* |
| **early voting** | 사전 투표 | *Millions have already cast ballots through early voting.* |
| **canvassing** | 지지자 방문 유세, 표심 확인 활동 | *Volunteers spent the weekend canvassing neighborhoods door-to-door.* |
| **approval rating** | 지지율 | *The president's approval rating has dropped five points since September.* |
| **generic ballot** | 일반 지지율 (정당별 선호도 조사) | *The generic ballot shows Democrats up by two points nationally.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"With Election Day just three weeks away, both parties are pouring resources into battleground states where Senate races remain too close to call."*

> *"Republicans are confident they can hold their current seats, but Democrats say a late surge in voter registration could change the map entirely."*

> *"Analysts warn that record early voting numbers make it harder than ever to predict which party will control the upper chamber come January."*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *too close to call* — 결과를 예측하기 어려운, 박빙의 승부
- *pour resources into* — ~에 자원을 쏟아붓다
- *come January* — 1월이 되면, 내년 1월에는 (미래 시점을 자연스럽게 표현할 때)
