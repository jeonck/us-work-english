## 이해도 확인

1. What is at stake in the 2026 midterm elections?
2. What is "down-ballot" and why does it matter?
3. What does the phrase "pulling out all the stops" mean in the broadcast excerpt?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. Control of both the Senate and the House of Representatives is at stake, which would determine the direction of the legislative agenda.
2. "Down-ballot" refers to candidates and races listed below the top of the ballot (such as Senate or House races listed below a governor's race). It matters because top-of-ticket popularity can either help or hurt these lower-profile candidates.
3. "Pulling out all the stops" means making every possible effort — doing everything you can to succeed. It comes from organ playing, where pulling out all the stops produces the maximum sound.

</details>

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *get out the vote* — 투표 참여를 독려하다 ("The campaign launched an aggressive get-out-the-vote drive.")
- *toss-up race* — 결과를 예측할 수 없는 접전 경쟁 ("Three Senate seats are rated as toss-ups by major forecasters.")
- *nail-biter* — 결과를 알 때까지 손에 땀을 쥐게 하는 상황 ("Election night is expected to be a real nail-biter.")
