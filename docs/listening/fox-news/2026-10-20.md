# 2026년 10월 20일 — Federal Reserve Holds Rates Amid Inflation Concerns

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Stands Pat on Interest Rates, Signals Cautious Path Forward as Core Inflation Remains Stubborn"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **stand pat** | 현상 유지하다, 변경하지 않다 | *The committee decided to stand pat on rates for the third consecutive meeting.* |
| **core inflation** | 근원 인플레이션 (식품·에너지 제외) | *Core inflation remains above the Fed's two-percent target.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *Monetary policy decisions affect borrowing costs for millions of Americans.* |
| **hawkish** | 매파적인 (금리 인상 선호) | *Several board members took a more hawkish stance at Tuesday's meeting.* |
| **dovish** | 비둘기파적인 (금리 인하 선호) | *Markets had expected a dovish pivot, but the Fed surprised investors.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate has been held steady at five-point-five percent.* |
| **yield curve** | 수익률 곡선 | *An inverted yield curve often signals an upcoming recession.* |
| **tighten the belt** | 긴축하다, 허리띠를 졸라매다 | *Households across the country are tightening their belts amid higher borrowing costs.* |
| **fiscal headwind** | 재정적 역풍 | *Rising deficits pose a fiscal headwind to economic growth.* |
| **labor market resilience** | 노동 시장의 탄력성 | *Labor market resilience has given the Fed room to keep rates elevated.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The Federal Reserve voted unanimously today to hold its benchmark interest rate steady, citing persistent inflationary pressures and a still-resilient labor market as reasons to maintain its cautious stance."*

> *"Fed Chair Jerome Powell told reporters the committee remains data-dependent, stressing that any future rate adjustments will hinge on incoming economic indicators rather than a preset timeline."*

> *"Wall Street responded with modest gains after the announcement, as investors interpreted the Fed's language as leaving the door open for a potential rate cut early next year, provided inflation continues its gradual downward trend."*

## 이해도 확인

1. Why did the Federal Reserve decide to hold interest rates steady?
2. What did Fed Chair Powell say about future rate changes?
3. How did Wall Street react to the Fed's announcement?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. The Fed cited persistent inflationary pressures and a resilient labor market as reasons to maintain its cautious stance.
2. Powell said the committee is data-dependent and that future adjustments will depend on incoming economic indicators, not a preset timeline.
3. Wall Street responded with modest gains, interpreting the Fed's language as leaving the door open for a potential rate cut early next year.

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *hold steady* — 변동 없이 유지하다
- *data-dependent* — 데이터에 근거한, 지표를 보고 판단하는
- *hinge on* — ~에 달려 있다, ~에 의존하다
- *leave the door open* — 가능성을 열어두다
- *rate cut* — 금리 인하
