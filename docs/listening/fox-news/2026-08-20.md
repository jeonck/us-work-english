# 2026년 08월 20일 — Federal Reserve Holds Interest Rates Steady

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Holds Rates Steady as Inflation Remains Stubborn, Markets Watch for Clues on Future Cuts"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **hold steady** | 유지하다, 안정시키다 | *The Fed voted to hold rates steady for the third consecutive meeting.* |
| **stubborn inflation** | 완강한 인플레이션 | *Stubborn inflation has kept policymakers from cutting rates.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *The committee debated the direction of monetary policy going forward.* |
| **hawkish** | 긴축적인, 매파적인 | *Several Fed officials gave hawkish signals at the press conference.* |
| **dovish** | 완화적인, 비둘기파적인 | *Markets had hoped for a more dovish tone from the chairman.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate remains at its highest level in two decades.* |
| **yield** | 수익률, 이율 | *Treasury yields fell slightly after the announcement.* |
| **recession fears** | 경기침체 우려 | *Recession fears are mounting as consumer spending slows.* |
| **data-dependent** | 데이터 의존적인 | *The Fed stressed it will remain data-dependent in future decisions.* |
| **soft landing** | 연착륙 | *Economists are debating whether the U.S. can achieve a soft landing.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The Federal Reserve voted unanimously today to hold its benchmark interest rate steady, signaling that policymakers need more evidence that inflation is sustainably moving toward their two-percent target."*

> *"Fed Chair Jerome Powell told reporters the committee remains committed to bringing inflation under control, but acknowledged that the path forward is uncertain, and that any future rate cuts will be firmly data-dependent."*

> *"Wall Street reacted cautiously to the announcement, with major indices closing mixed as investors parsed the Fed's statement for clues about the timing of potential rate reductions later this year."*

## 이해도 확인

1. What did the Federal Reserve decide to do with interest rates at today's meeting?
2. What condition did Fed Chair Powell say must be met before the Fed considers cutting rates?
3. How did Wall Street react to the Fed's announcement?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. The Federal Reserve voted to hold interest rates steady (unchanged).
2. Powell said the Fed needs more evidence that inflation is sustainably moving toward the two-percent target before considering rate cuts.
3. Wall Street reacted cautiously, with major indices closing mixed as investors looked for clues about the timing of future rate cuts.

</details>

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *interest rate hike* — 금리 인상 (반대: *interest rate cut* 금리 인하)
- *tighten monetary policy* — 통화 정책을 긴축하다
- *ease monetary policy* — 통화 정책을 완화하다
- *core inflation* — 근원 인플레이션 (에너지·식품 제외)
- *consumer price index (CPI)* — 소비자 물가지수
- *purchasing power* — 구매력
