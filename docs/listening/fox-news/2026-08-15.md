# 2026년 08월 15일 — Fed Signals Rate Cut as Inflation Cools

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Federal Reserve Chair signals potential interest rate cut as inflation data shows sustained cooling toward 2% target"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **sustained cooling** | 지속적인 냉각(물가 하락) | *The sustained cooling of inflation gave the Fed room to act.* |
| **rate cut** | 금리 인하 | *Markets rallied after the Fed hinted at a rate cut.* |
| **pivot** | 정책 전환 | *Investors have long awaited a Fed pivot toward easing.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate has held steady for over a year.* |
| **monetary easing** | 통화 완화 | *Monetary easing could boost borrowing and consumer spending.* |
| **core inflation** | 근원 물가 상승률 | *Core inflation, which strips out food and energy, fell to 2.1%.* |
| **labor market** | 노동 시장 | *A resilient labor market complicates the timing of rate cuts.* |
| **yield curve** | 수익률 곡선 | *The yield curve steepened after the Fed Chair's remarks.* |
| **dovish stance** | 비둘기파적 입장 (금리 인하 선호) | *The Chair adopted a more dovish stance than expected.* |
| **fiscal policy** | 재정 정책 | *Fiscal policy from Congress also weighs on inflation forecasts.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The Federal Reserve Chair told Congress today that sustained progress on inflation has opened the door to potential interest rate cuts in the coming months."*

> *"Core inflation has now held below two-point-three percent for three consecutive months, giving policymakers growing confidence that price pressures have been tamed."*

> *"Wall Street surged on the news, with the S&P 500 jumping nearly one-point-eight percent as traders priced in a higher probability of a September rate cut."*

## 이해도 확인

1. Why is the Federal Reserve considering an interest rate cut?
2. What does "core inflation" measure, and why is it significant?
3. How did Wall Street react to the Fed Chair's remarks?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. Because inflation has shown sustained cooling, holding below the 2% target range for several consecutive months, giving the Fed confidence that price pressures are under control.
2. Core inflation measures price changes excluding food and energy — volatile categories — making it a cleaner signal of underlying inflation trends. It's significant because the Fed focuses on it when setting policy.
3. Wall Street surged, with the S&P 500 rising nearly 1.8%, as traders increased their bets on a September rate cut.

</details>
