# 2026년 08월 14일 — Federal Reserve Holds Rates Amid Inflation Debate

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Signals No Rate Cuts in Sight as Consumer Prices Remain Stubbornly High"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **hold rates** | 금리를 동결하다 | *The Fed voted to hold rates for the third consecutive meeting.* |
| **stubbornly high** | 완고하게 높은, 좀처럼 내려가지 않는 | *Inflation remained stubbornly high despite tighter monetary policy.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *The committee debated shifting its monetary policy stance.* |
| **rate cut** | 금리 인하 | *Wall Street had been pricing in a rate cut by September.* |
| **consumer prices** | 소비자 물가 | *Consumer prices rose 3.2% year-over-year last month.* |
| **hawkish** | 매파적인 (긴축 선호) | *Several Fed officials maintained a hawkish tone in their remarks.* |
| **dovish** | 비둘기파적인 (완화 선호) | *Markets were hoping for a more dovish signal from the chairman.* |
| **unanimous vote** | 만장일치 투표 | *The decision to hold was a unanimous vote among committee members.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate has stayed above 5% for over a year.* |
| **press conference** | 기자 회견 | *The Fed chair addressed reporters at a press conference after the decision.* |
| **data-dependent** | 데이터에 따라 결정되는 | *Officials stressed they remain data-dependent on future moves.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The Federal Reserve held interest rates steady today, signaling that policymakers are in no rush to cut borrowing costs as inflation remains above their two-percent target."*

> *"Fed Chair Jerome Powell told reporters the committee needs to see more convincing evidence that inflation is sustainably moving lower before it considers easing its restrictive policy stance."*

> *"Wall Street reacted with disappointment, with the Dow falling more than three hundred points following the announcement, as investors had been hoping for clearer guidance on when cuts might begin."*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *price in* — (기대치를) 가격에 반영하다 ("Markets had already priced in a cut.")
- *tighten monetary policy* — 통화 정책을 긴축하다 ("The Fed moved to tighten monetary policy aggressively in 2022.")
- *ease borrowing costs* — 차입 비용을 낮추다 ("Businesses are waiting for the Fed to ease borrowing costs.")
