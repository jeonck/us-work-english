# 2026년 09월 15일 — Federal Reserve Holds Rates Amid Inflation Concerns

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Keeps Interest Rates Unchanged, Signals Cautious Approach to Future Cuts"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **hold rates** | 금리를 동결하다 | *The Fed voted to hold rates at the current level.* |
| **inflation** | 인플레이션, 물가상승 | *Inflation remains above the 2% target.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *Monetary policy decisions affect borrowing costs nationwide.* |

| **labor market** | 노동 시장 | *The labor market remains resilient despite economic headwinds.* |
| **yield** | 수익률, 채권 이율 | *Treasury yields rose sharply after the Fed's announcement.* |
| **tighten (monetary policy)** | 긴축하다 | *The Fed was forced to tighten policy to combat rising prices.* |
| **economic headwinds** | 경제적 역풍 | *Global economic headwinds are weighing on growth projections.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *The Federal Reserve voted unanimously today to hold interest rates steady, keeping the benchmark federal funds rate in its current range as policymakers weigh persistent inflation against signs of a cooling economy.*

> *Fed Chair Jerome Powell signaled that while progress on inflation has been made, the central bank is not yet confident enough to begin cutting rates, citing continued strength in the labor market and elevated core prices.*

> *Wall Street responded with mixed signals — stocks initially dipped before recovering, while Treasury yields climbed as investors recalibrated their expectations for rate cuts later this year.*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *data-dependent* — 데이터에 따라 결정하는 (Fed가 자주 쓰는 표현: "We are data-dependent")
- *rate cut expectations* — 금리 인하 기대감
- *dovish stance* — 비둘기파적 입장 (금리 인하 선호, hawkish의 반대)
- *cooling economy* — 냉각되는 경제, 경기 둔화
- *core inflation* — 근원 물가상승률 (식품·에너지 제외)
