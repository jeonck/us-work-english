## 이해도 확인

1. What does the phrase "ground game" refer to in the context of an election campaign?
2. Why are analysts saying voter-turnout infrastructure is especially important this cycle?
3. What does it mean when a race is "within the margin of error"?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. "Ground game" refers to direct voter-contact operations such as door-knocking, phone banking, and driving voters to the polls — as opposed to TV ads or digital campaigns.
2. Because the race is extremely competitive, the party that can more effectively mobilize its own supporters to actually vote could tip the balance in close states.
3. When a race is within the margin of error, the statistical difference between the candidates is so small that the poll cannot reliably predict who is ahead — essentially, it is a statistical tie.

</details>

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *in full swing* — 한창 진행 중인 ("The campaign season is now in full swing.")
- *smash records* — 기록을 깨다 ("Early-voting numbers smashed previous midterm records.")
- *come January* — 1월이 되면, 1월에는 ("A new Congress will be seated come January.")
- *make or break* — 성공 아니면 실패를 결정하다 ("This debate could make or break his campaign.")
- *the final stretch* — 마지막 단계, 막판 ("Both sides are leaving nothing to chance in the final stretch.")
