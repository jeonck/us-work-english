# 2026년 08월 16일 — Fed Signals Rate Cut as Inflation Cools

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"FED CHAIR SIGNALS IMMINENT RATE CUT AS INFLATION DATA COMES IN COOLER THAN EXPECTED"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **imminent** | 곧 닥칠, 임박한 | *An imminent rate cut could boost consumer spending.* |
| **rate cut** | 금리 인하 | *The Fed announced a rate cut of 25 basis points.* |
| **inflation** | 인플레이션, 물가 상승 | *Inflation has been cooling steadily for several months.* |
| **basis points** | 베이시스 포인트 (0.01%) | *The central bank reduced rates by 50 basis points.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *Tighter monetary policy was used to fight inflation.* |
| **consumer confidence** | 소비자 신뢰 지수 | *Consumer confidence rose following the Fed's announcement.* |
| **dovish** | 비둘기파적 (금리 인하 선호) | *The Fed chair's remarks were seen as increasingly dovish.* |
| **pivot** | 방향 전환 | *Markets are betting on a policy pivot toward easing.* |
| **yield** | 수익률 | *Treasury yields fell sharply on the news.* |
| **softening** | 약화, 완화 | *A softening labor market supports the case for cuts.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell signaled today that the central bank is ready to cut interest rates, saying the time has come to adjust policy as inflation moves closer to the two-percent target.*

> *The latest consumer price index data showed inflation cooling to two-point-three percent year-over-year, giving policymakers more room to ease borrowing costs for businesses and households.*

> *Wall Street surged on the news, with the S&P 500 climbing nearly one percent as investors priced in at least two rate cuts before the end of the year.*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *ease borrowing costs* — 대출 비용을 낮추다
- *price in* — (시장이) 반영하다, 예상하여 가격에 포함시키다
- *year-over-year* — 전년 동기 대비
- *tighten / ease monetary policy* — 통화 정책을 긴축 / 완화하다
- *the time has come to ~* — ~할 때가 왔다
