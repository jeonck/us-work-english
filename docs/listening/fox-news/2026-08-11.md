# 2026년 08월 11일 — Fed Signals Rate Cuts as Inflation Eases

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Federal Reserve Signals Back-to-Back Rate Cuts as Inflation Drops to Two-Year Low"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *The Fed's monetary policy has shifted in a more dovish direction.* |
| **rate cut** | 금리 인하 | *Markets are pricing in two rate cuts before year-end.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준금리 | *The benchmark rate currently stands at four and a half percent.* |
| **dovish** | (금리에 대해) 완화적인, 비둘기파의 | *Fed Chair delivered a notably dovish speech at the annual summit.* |
| **inflation eases** | 인플레이션이 완화되다 | *Inflation eased to its lowest level in two years last month.* |
| **labor market** | 노동 시장 | *A cooling labor market gave policymakers more room to act.* |
| **yield** | (채권) 수익률 | *Treasury yields fell sharply following the Fed's announcement.* |
| **recession fears** | 경기 침체 우려 | *Recession fears have begun to ease as consumer spending holds up.* |
| **pivot** | (정책) 전환 | *Wall Street welcomed the Fed's long-awaited policy pivot.* |
| **gross domestic product (GDP)** | 국내총생산 | *GDP growth slowed to one point two percent in the second quarter.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The Federal Reserve is widely expected to cut interest rates at its September meeting, after fresh data showed inflation cooling to its lowest level in more than two years."*

> *"Fed Chair said in a statement that the central bank is now confident inflation is moving sustainably toward its two-percent target, opening the door to a policy pivot."*

> *"Wall Street surged on the news, with the Dow Jones Industrial Average climbing more than four hundred points in afternoon trading."*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *price in* — (시장이) 미리 반영하다 ("Markets have already priced in a rate cut.")
- *hold steady* — 현 수준을 유지하다 ("The Fed voted to hold rates steady last month.")
- *tighten monetary policy* — 통화 정책을 긴축하다 ("The Fed spent two years tightening monetary policy to fight inflation.")
