# 2026년 09월 07일 — Federal Reserve Signals Rate Cut Amid Economic Slowdown

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Chair Signals Imminent Rate Cut as Labor Market Cools and Inflation Retreats to Target"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **imminent** | 임박한, 곧 닥칠 | *An imminent rate cut could boost consumer spending.* |
| **labor market** | 노동 시장 | *The labor market has cooled significantly over the past quarter.* |
| **inflation** | 인플레이션, 물가 상승 | *Inflation has retreated to the Fed's 2% target.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *The Fed adjusts monetary policy to stabilize the economy.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate has held at 4.5% for six months.* |
| **dovish** | 완화적인 (금리 인하 선호) | *The Fed Chair struck a dovish tone in her remarks.* |
| **pivot** | 정책 전환 | *Wall Street is anticipating a Fed pivot toward easing.* |
| **recession fears** | 경기 침체 우려 | *Recession fears have mounted as growth slows.* |
| **yield** | 수익률, 금리 | *Treasury yields fell sharply on the news.* |
| **soft landing** | 연착륙 (경기 급격한 침체 없는 안정) | *Economists debated whether a soft landing is still achievable.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *Federal Reserve Chair signaled Friday that the central bank is ready to begin cutting interest rates, citing a meaningful cooling in the labor market and inflation now running close to its 2% target.*

> *Markets rallied on the news, with the S&P 500 climbing more than one percent as investors priced in a rate cut at the Fed's September meeting — what many on Wall Street are calling the long-awaited pivot.*

> *Still, some economists urge caution, warning that cutting too quickly could reignite inflation, while waiting too long risks tipping the economy into the recession that policymakers have worked so hard to avoid.*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *price in* — (시장이) 이미 반영하다 ("Markets have already priced in a rate cut.")
- *tipping point* — 임계점, 전환점 ("The economy may be nearing a tipping point.")
- *hold steady* — 동결하다, 유지하다 ("The Fed decided to hold steady on rates this quarter.")
- *ease monetary policy* — 통화 정책을 완화하다 ("The central bank is expected to ease monetary policy.")
- *hawkish vs. dovish* — 매파(금리 인상 선호) 대 비둘기파(금리 인하 선호) ("The committee is split between hawkish and dovish members.")
