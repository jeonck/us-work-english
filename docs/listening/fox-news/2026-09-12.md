# 2026년 09월 12일 — Federal Reserve Holds Interest Rates Steady Amid Economic Uncertainty

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"The Federal Reserve kept its benchmark interest rate unchanged for the third consecutive meeting, signaling a cautious approach as policymakers weigh inflation pressures against slowing economic growth."**

## 이해도 확인

1. Why did the Federal Reserve keep interest rates unchanged?
2. What does "data-dependent" mean in the context of monetary policy?
3. How did the stock market respond to the Fed's announcement?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. The Fed held rates steady due to uncertainty — balancing ongoing inflation concerns with signs of slowing economic growth, adopting a cautious wait-and-see approach.
2. "Data-dependent" means the Fed will base future rate decisions on incoming economic data (such as inflation reports and employment numbers) rather than a preset schedule.
3. Markets reacted cautiously — the S&P 500 edged slightly lower as investors absorbed the Fed's decision to hold rates and its non-committal forward guidance.

</details>
