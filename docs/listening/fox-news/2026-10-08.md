# 2026년 10월 08일 — U.S. Braces for Hurricane Milton as Florida Declares Emergency

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Category 4 Hurricane Milton Barrels Toward Florida Gulf Coast; Mandatory Evacuations Ordered"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **barrel toward** | ~을 향해 빠르게 돌진하다 | *The storm is barreling toward the Gulf Coast at over 130 mph.* |
| **mandatory evacuation** | 강제 대피령 | *Authorities issued mandatory evacuations for all coastal zones.* |
| **landfall** | 상륙 (허리케인이 육지에 닿는 것) | *Milton is expected to make landfall late Wednesday night.* |
| **storm surge** | 폭풍 해일 | *A life-threatening storm surge of up to 15 feet is forecast.* |
| **brace for** | ~에 대비하다 | *Residents are bracing for what could be a historic storm.* |
| **state of emergency** | 비상사태 | *The governor declared a state of emergency across 51 counties.* |
| **catastrophic** | 대재앙적인, 치명적인 | *Officials warn of catastrophic wind damage along the coast.* |
| **shelter in place** | 실내 대피 (이동하지 않고 건물 안에 머무르기) | *Those who cannot evacuate are urged to shelter in place.* |
| **infrastructure** | 사회 기반 시설 | *The hurricane could knock out critical infrastructure for weeks.* |
| **FEMA** | 연방재난관리청 | *FEMA has pre-positioned supplies and rescue teams across the region.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"Hurricane Milton is roaring toward the Florida Gulf Coast as a Category 4 storm, with winds topping 140 miles per hour and a potentially catastrophic storm surge threatening low-lying communities."*

> *"Millions of residents are under mandatory evacuation orders tonight, as Florida's governor warns this could be one of the most destructive storms to hit the state in decades."*

> *"FEMA and the National Guard are on the ground, pre-positioning supplies and rescue teams ahead of what forecasters say could be a direct hit on the Tampa Bay area."*

## 이해도 확인

1. What category is Hurricane Milton, and what makes it particularly dangerous?
2. What action did Florida's governor take ahead of the storm's arrival?
3. How is the federal government preparing for the hurricane?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. Milton is a Category 4 hurricane with winds over 140 mph; it is especially dangerous because of a potentially catastrophic storm surge threatening coastal communities.
2. The governor declared a state of emergency and issued mandatory evacuation orders for millions of residents across coastal zones.
3. FEMA pre-positioned supplies and rescue teams, and the National Guard has been deployed across the region ahead of the expected landfall.

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *make landfall* — (태풍·허리케인이) 상륙하다 (e.g. *The storm made landfall near Tampa at midnight.*)
- *in the path of* — ~의 진로에 있는 (e.g. *Thousands of homes are directly in the path of the hurricane.*)
- *brace for impact* — 충격에 대비하다 (e.g. *The entire state is bracing for impact as Milton approaches.*)
