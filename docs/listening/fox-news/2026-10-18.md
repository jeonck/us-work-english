# 2026년 10월 18일 — Republicans Focus on Economy as Midterm Elections Near

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"GOP Hammers Democrats on Inflation, Border as Midterms Close In"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **hammer** | (정치적으로) 강하게 공격하다 | *Republicans are hammering Democrats over rising grocery prices.* |
| **close in** | 다가오다, 임박하다 | *With election day closing in, both parties are ramping up their campaigns.* |
| **ramp up** | 강화하다, 증가시키다 | *The campaign is ramping up its ground game in key swing states.* |
| **swing state** | 경합 주 (선거 결과가 불확실한 주) | *Pennsylvania is considered the most critical swing state this cycle.* |
| **ground game** | 풀뿌리 선거 운동 (유권자 직접 접촉 활동) | *A strong ground game can make the difference in a tight race.* |
| **tight race** | 접전, 박빙 경쟁 | *Polls show a tight race in several Senate seats across the country.* |
| **incumbent** | 현직 (의원, 관리 등) | *The incumbent senator is trailing in the latest polls.* |
| **polling average** | 여론조사 평균 | *According to the polling average, the Republican candidate leads by three points.* |
| **fiscal policy** | 재정 정책 | *Voters are asking candidates to clarify their stance on fiscal policy.* |
| **cost of living** | 생활 물가 | *The cost of living crisis is the top concern for most American families.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"With just three weeks until Election Day, Republican candidates are making the economy their central message, pointing to stubborn inflation and record-high grocery bills as proof that Democrats have failed the American middle class."*

> *"The polling average in several battleground states shows a tightening race, with GOP challengers gaining ground on incumbent Democrats who have struggled to defend their economic record."*

> *"Party strategists say the ground game will be crucial — candidates are deploying volunteers door-to-door in swing districts, urging voters to show up on November third."*

## 이해도 확인

1. What is the main message that Republican candidates are focusing on ahead of the midterms?
2. What does "ground game" mean in the context of a political campaign?
3. What do "battleground states" or "swing states" refer to in American elections?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. Republican candidates are focusing on the economy — specifically inflation and high grocery prices — as their central campaign message.
2. "Ground game" refers to direct voter outreach activities like door-knocking, phone banking, and mobilizing volunteers to contact voters face-to-face.
3. Battleground states (also called swing states) are states where neither party has a clear advantage and the election outcome is uncertain, making them critical to winning an election.

</details>

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *make or break* — 성패를 결정짓는 ("The final debate could be a make-or-break moment for the campaign.")
