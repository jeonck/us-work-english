# 2026년 10월 03일 — Federal Reserve Holds Rates Amid Mixed Economic Signals

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Keeps Benchmark Rate Unchanged as Policymakers Weigh Inflation Data Against Slowing Job Growth"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **benchmark rate** | 기준금리 | *The Fed kept the benchmark rate at its current level for the third consecutive meeting.* |
| **hold steady** | 유지하다, 변동 없이 지속하다 | *Officials decided to hold steady on monetary policy until more data comes in.* |
| **policymaker** | 정책 입안자 | *Policymakers are divided on whether a rate cut is appropriate this quarter.* |

| **yield curve** | 수익률 곡선 | *The inverted yield curve has many analysts warning of a potential slowdown ahead.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

> *The Federal Reserve voted unanimously on Wednesday to hold its benchmark interest rate steady, signaling that officials want to see further progress on inflation before making any additional adjustments.*

> *Chairman Jerome Powell told reporters that the labor market remains resilient, but policymakers are keeping a close eye on consumer spending data as higher borrowing costs continue to weigh on American households.*

> *Wall Street responded cautiously to the announcement, with major indexes finishing the session mixed as traders recalibrated their expectations for a rate cut later in the year.*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *keep a close eye on* — ~을 면밀히 주시하다 (= monitor carefully)
- *weigh on* — ~에 부담을 주다, 압박하다
- *recalibrate expectations* — 기대치를 재조정하다
- *hold the line* — 현 상태를 유지하다, 입장을 고수하다
- *come in* — (데이터·결과가) 나오다, 집계되다 (e.g., *until more data comes in*)
