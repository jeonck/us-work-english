# 2026년 09월 21일 — Midterm Elections: Economy and Immigration Dominate Debate

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Republicans Tout Economic Record, Push Immigration Overhaul as Midterms Near"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **tout** | 자랑하다, 선전하다 | *The candidate touted his record on job creation.* |
| **overhaul** | 전면 개편, 대대적 수리 | *Lawmakers called for a complete overhaul of immigration policy.* |
| **midterm elections** | 중간선거 | *Midterm elections are held two years into a presidential term.* |
| **incumbent** | 현직자, 재직 중인 | *The incumbent senator faces a strong challenger this fall.* |
| **galvanize** | 자극하다, 분발시키다 | *Rising prices galvanized voters to head to the polls.* |
| **border security** | 국경 안보 | *Border security remains a top concern for many Americans.* |
| **fiscal responsibility** | 재정 책임 | *The party ran on a platform of fiscal responsibility.* |
| **swing district** | 경합 지역 | *Candidates in swing districts are moderating their tone.* |
| **grassroots** | 풀뿌리, 일반 대중 | *A grassroots campaign mobilized thousands of volunteers.* |
| **endorsement** | 지지 선언, 공인 | *The governor's endorsement could prove decisive in the primary.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"With the November midterm elections now less than seven weeks away, Republican candidates across the country are doubling down on their core message: cut spending, secure the border, and put more money back in Americans' pockets."*

> *"Polls show the economy remains the number-one issue for voters, with inflation and housing costs still squeezing middle-class families despite a series of interest rate cuts from the Federal Reserve."*

> *"In key swing districts from Arizona to Pennsylvania, party strategists say the race will come down to turnout — and whichever side does a better job of galvanizing its base in the final weeks will likely carry the House."*

## 이해도 확인

1. What two issues are Republicans emphasizing as the midterms approach?
2. What does the poll data say is the top concern for voters?
3. According to party strategists, what will decide the House races?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. Republicans are focusing on cutting spending / border security and economic relief (putting money back in Americans' pockets).
2. The economy — specifically inflation and housing costs — is the number-one issue for voters.
3. Turnout will decide the House races; whichever party better galvanizes its base in the final weeks is expected to win.
