# 2026년 09월 25일 — Federal Reserve Signals Rate Cuts Amid Economic Slowdown

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Chair signals aggressive rate cuts as GDP growth slows to near zero, raising fears of a looming recession."**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **signal** | 신호를 보내다, 암시하다 | *The Fed chair signaled a shift in monetary policy.* |
| **rate cut** | 금리 인하 | *Analysts expect a series of rate cuts before year-end.* |
| **economic slowdown** | 경기 둔화 | *The economic slowdown has affected hiring across all sectors.* |
| **GDP (gross domestic product)** | 국내총생산 | *GDP growth fell to 0.2% in the third quarter.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *The Fed adjusts monetary policy to stabilize the economy.* |
| **recession** | 경기 침체 | *Two consecutive quarters of negative growth define a recession.* |
| **looming** | 임박한, 다가오는 | *A looming recession has rattled investors on Wall Street.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준금리 | *The benchmark rate currently stands at 4.75%.* |
| **inflation** | 인플레이션, 물가 상승 | *Inflation has eased significantly over the past year.* |
| **labor market** | 노동 시장 | *A cooling labor market is one sign of slowing growth.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell hinted today that the central bank is prepared to cut interest rates aggressively if economic conditions continue to deteriorate.*

> *GDP growth has slowed to near zero, and a string of weak jobs reports has fueled speculation that the U.S. economy could slip into a recession before the end of the year.*

> *Wall Street responded with a sharp rally, with the Dow Jones Industrial Average surging over 400 points as investors bet on cheaper borrowing costs ahead.*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *rattle investors* — 투자자들을 불안하게 만들다 (e.g., *Weak data rattled investors across global markets.*)
- *bet on* — ~에 베팅하다, ~을 예상하다 (e.g., *Traders are betting on a half-point rate cut next month.*)
- *slip into a recession* — 경기 침체에 빠져들다 (e.g., *Policymakers are trying to prevent the economy from slipping into a recession.*)
