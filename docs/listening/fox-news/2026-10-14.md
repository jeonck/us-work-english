# 2026년 10월 14일 — Senate Debates Border Security Funding

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Senate Showdown: Lawmakers Clash Over Emergency Border Security Appropriations Amid Record Crossing Numbers"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **appropriations** | 예산 배정, 세출 | *Congress passed the defense appropriations bill last week.* |
| **bipartisan** | 초당적인, 양당 합의의 | *The infrastructure deal had bipartisan support.* |
| **surge** | 급증, 급격히 오르다 | *Illegal crossings surged to record levels this quarter.* |
| **detain / detention** | 구금하다 / 구금 | *Migrants were detained at the southern border facility.* |
| **filibuster** | 의사 진행 방해 | *The minority party threatened a filibuster to block the vote.* |
| **allocate** | 할당하다, 배분하다 | *The administration plans to allocate $4 billion for new barriers.* |
| **enforcement** | 집행, 단속 | *Border enforcement operations intensified over the weekend.* |
| **standoff** | 교착 상태, 대치 | *The budget standoff between the two chambers continued.* |
| **expedite** | 신속히 처리하다 | *Lawmakers want to expedite deportation proceedings.* |
| **sovereignty** | 주권 | *Critics say lax border control undermines national sovereignty.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"Senate leaders are locked in a bitter standoff tonight over a proposed eight-billion-dollar emergency spending package aimed at surging resources to the southern border."*

> *"Republican lawmakers argue the funds are urgently needed to detain record numbers of migrants and expedite deportation proceedings, while Democrats insist stricter enforcement alone won't solve the underlying crisis."*

> *"With a government funding deadline just two weeks away, both sides are under intense pressure to reach a bipartisan deal — or risk a partial shutdown that would furlough thousands of federal workers."*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *locked in a standoff* — 교착 상태에 빠진 ("The two parties are locked in a standoff over the budget.")
- *underlying crisis* — 근본적인 위기 ("Enforcement alone won't solve the underlying crisis.")
- *funding deadline* — 예산 마감 시한 ("The funding deadline is approaching fast.")
- *furlough workers* — 직원들을 일시 해고하다 ("A shutdown would furlough thousands of federal workers.")
- *intensify operations* — 작전을 강화하다 ("Border enforcement operations intensified over the weekend.")
