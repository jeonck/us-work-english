> **"White House Signals Progress in US-China Trade Talks as Tariff Tensions Weigh on Global Markets"**

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The White House confirmed Tuesday that high-level trade talks with Beijing are back on the table, signaling a potential thaw in relations after months of escalating tariff disputes."*

> *"Treasury Secretary Stevens called the discussions 'constructive,' though both sides acknowledged that significant sticking points remain, including market access for American firms and Beijing's state subsidies to domestic manufacturers."*

> *"Analysts warn that without a comprehensive deal, continued tariff pressure could slow US economic growth by as much as half a percentage point heading into the midterm election season."*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *back on the table* — 다시 협상 테이블에 올라온, 재개된 ("The proposal is back on the table after last week's breakdown.")
- *thaw in relations* — 관계의 해빙, 냉각된 관계가 풀리는 것 ("A thaw in relations between the two nations is long overdue.")
- *escalating tensions* — 고조되는 긴장 ("Escalating tensions in the region are making investors nervous.")
