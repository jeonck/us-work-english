# 2026년 09월 01일 — Federal Reserve Holds Rates Amid Inflation Concerns

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Keeps Interest Rates Steady as Inflation Battle Continues"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **hold steady** | 현상 유지하다, 변동 없이 유지하다 | *The Fed chose to hold rates steady for the third consecutive meeting.* |
| **inflation** | 인플레이션, 물가 상승 | *Core inflation remains above the Fed's 2% target.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준금리 | *The benchmark rate is currently set at 4.5%.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *Monetary policy decisions affect borrowing costs across the economy.* |
| **hawkish** | 매파적인 (금리 인상 선호) | *Several Fed officials took a hawkish stance in the latest meeting.* |
| **dovish** | 비둘기파적인 (금리 인하 선호) | *Markets were hoping for a more dovish signal from the Fed chair.* |
| **labor market** | 노동 시장 | *A resilient labor market complicates the Fed's path to rate cuts.* |
| **consumer spending** | 소비자 지출 | *Consumer spending remains robust despite elevated borrowing costs.* |
| **data-dependent** | 데이터에 따라 결정하는 | *The Fed stressed it remains data-dependent in its approach.* |
| **rate cut** | 금리 인하 | *Investors are pricing in one rate cut before the end of the year.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *The Federal Reserve voted unanimously on Wednesday to hold interest rates steady, leaving the benchmark federal funds rate at its current target range as policymakers wait for clearer signs that inflation is durably cooling.*

> *Fed Chair stated that while inflation has made meaningful progress, the central bank is not yet confident enough to begin cutting rates, emphasizing that officials remain committed to returning inflation to the two-percent target.*

> *Financial markets reacted with mild disappointment, as many investors had hoped for stronger signals of an imminent rate reduction. The S&P 500 dipped slightly following the announcement, while Treasury yields ticked higher.*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *tighten monetary policy* — 통화 정책을 긴축하다 (금리 올려 경기 억제)
- *ease monetary policy* — 통화 정책을 완화하다 (금리 내려 경기 부양)
- *price stability* — 물가 안정 (연준의 핵심 목표 중 하나)
- *soft landing* — 연착륙 (경기 침체 없이 인플레이션을 잡는 시나리오)
- *yield curve* — 수익률 곡선 (단기·장기 국채 금리 간의 관계)
