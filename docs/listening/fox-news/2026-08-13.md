# 2026년 08월 13일 — Federal Reserve Signals Rate Cuts Amid Cooling Inflation

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Chair Signals Possible Rate Cuts as Inflation Nears Target, Boosting Market Optimism"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **signal** | 신호를 보내다, 암시하다 | *The Fed chair signaled a shift in monetary policy.* |
| **rate cut** | 금리 인하 | *Wall Street rallied on hopes of a rate cut next quarter.* |
| **inflation** | 인플레이션, 물가 상승 | *Cooling inflation gave the Fed room to act.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *The committee debated monetary policy for several hours.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate has remained unchanged for months.* |
| **consumer confidence** | 소비자 신뢰지수 | *Consumer confidence rose to its highest level this year.* |
| **yield** | 수익률 (채권 등) | *Treasury yields fell sharply after the announcement.* |
| **rally** | (시장이) 상승하다, 반등하다 | *Stock markets rallied following the Fed's statement.* |
| **fiscal policy** | 재정 정책 | *Analysts say fiscal policy must support the Fed's moves.* |
| **dovish** | 비둘기파적인 (금리 인하 선호) | *The Fed's tone was notably more dovish than expected.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell hinted today that the central bank could begin cutting interest rates as early as next month, citing significant progress in the battle against inflation."*

> *"The consumer price index rose just two-point-one percent year over year in July — its lowest reading since the Fed began its aggressive rate-hiking campaign three years ago."*

> *"Wall Street responded enthusiastically, with the Dow Jones Industrial Average closing up more than four hundred points as investors welcomed the prospect of cheaper borrowing costs."*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *cite* — (근거로) 언급하다 ("The official cited new data to support the decision.")
- *in the wake of* — ~의 여파로 ("Markets surged in the wake of the announcement.")
- *tighten / ease monetary policy* — 통화 정책을 긴축/완화하다 ("The Fed plans to ease monetary policy gradually.")
