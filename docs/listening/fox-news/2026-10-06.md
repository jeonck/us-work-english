## 이해도 확인

1. What term describes states where both parties compete fiercely for Senate seats?
2. What does it mean when a party is said to "flip" a seat?
3. What phrase means ads broadcast on television and radio during a campaign?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. Battleground states (or swing states).
2. It means the party wins a seat previously held by the opposing party.
3. "Flooding the airwaves" — refers to running large volumes of TV and radio advertisements.

</details>

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *too close to call* — 결과를 예측하기 어렵다 (It's too close to call — either candidate could win.)
- *mobilize the base* — 지지층을 결집시키다 (The party needs to mobilize its base before Election Day.)
- *concede defeat* — 패배를 인정하다 (The candidate called his opponent to concede defeat on election night.)
