## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *losing steam* — 동력을 잃다, 속도가 떨어지다 (The economy is losing steam after years of growth.)
- *well below* — 훨씬 못 미치는, 크게 하회하는 (Sales came in well below projections.)
- *on the table* — 논의·고려 대상인 (All options are on the table at this point.)
- *bet on* — ~에 베팅하다, ~을 예상하다 (Traders are betting on a 50-basis-point cut.)
- *falling short of* — ~에 미치지 못하다 (The report fell short of expectations once again.)
