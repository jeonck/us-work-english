# 2026년 09월 22일 — Fed Holds Rates Steady Amid Economic Uncertainty

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Federal Reserve Holds Interest Rates Steady, Signals Cautious Approach as Inflation Remains Stubborn"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **hold steady** | 현상 유지하다, 변동 없이 유지하다 | *The Fed decided to hold rates steady for the third consecutive meeting.* |
| **interest rate** | 금리 | *Rising interest rates make mortgages more expensive for homebuyers.* |
| **inflation** | 인플레이션, 물가 상승 | *Stubborn inflation has forced the central bank to reconsider its timeline.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *The committee discussed a shift in monetary policy at the closed-door meeting.* |
| **cautious approach** | 신중한 접근 방식 | *Officials emphasized a cautious approach given the mixed economic signals.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate remains at its highest level in more than two decades.* |
| **economic outlook** | 경제 전망 | *The Fed revised its economic outlook downward for the coming quarters.* |
| **consensus** | 합의, 공통된 의견 | *There was no clear consensus among policymakers on the next course of action.* |
| **resilient** | 회복력 있는, 탄탄한 | *The job market has remained surprisingly resilient despite tighter credit conditions.* |
| **dovish / hawkish** | 비둘기파 (완화적) / 매파 (긴축적) | *Several dovish members pushed for an early rate cut, while hawkish officials urged patience.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

> *"The Federal Reserve voted unanimously today to hold its benchmark interest rate steady, citing ongoing concerns about inflation that has remained above its two-percent target for over a year."*

> *"Fed Chair Jerome Powell told reporters that while the labor market continues to show remarkable resilience, policymakers need more convincing data before moving forward with any rate cuts."*

> *"Markets responded cautiously to the announcement, with the Dow Jones slipping nearly half a percent as investors digested the Fed's wait-and-see stance."*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *wait-and-see stance* — 상황을 지켜보는 자세, 관망 태도
- *above target* — 목표치를 상회하는 (e.g., inflation above target)
- *tighten monetary policy* — 통화 정책을 긴축하다 (금리 인상 등)
- *cut rates* — 금리를 인하하다
- *digest the news* — 뉴스를 소화하다, 뉴스의 의미를 파악하다
