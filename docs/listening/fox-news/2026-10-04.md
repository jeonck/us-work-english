# 2026년 10월 04일 — Fed Holds Rates Steady Amid Stubborn Inflation

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Federal Reserve holds interest rates steady for a third straight meeting as policymakers wrestle with stubborn inflation and slowing growth."**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **hold steady** | 유지하다, 변동 없이 버티다 | *The Fed decided to hold rates steady despite pressure to cut.* |
| **stubborn inflation** | 완고한 인플레이션 (좀처럼 잡히지 않는) | *Stubborn inflation has forced the central bank to delay rate cuts.* |
| **policymaker** | 정책 입안자 | *Policymakers are divided on when to begin easing monetary policy.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *Tight monetary policy has slowed consumer spending significantly.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준금리 | *The benchmark rate remains at its highest level in over two decades.* |
| **economic headwinds** | 경제적 역풍, 불리한 여건 | *Small businesses are facing significant economic headwinds this quarter.* |
| **tighten the screws** | 압박을 강화하다 | *Higher borrowing costs tighten the screws on mortgage applicants.* |
| **pivot** | (정책) 방향 전환 | *Wall Street is waiting for the Fed to pivot toward rate cuts.* |
| **core inflation** | 근원 인플레이션 (에너지·식품 제외) | *Core inflation came in hotter than expected at 3.4 percent.* |
| **rate hike** | 금리 인상 | *Economists no longer expect another rate hike this cycle.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *The Federal Reserve voted unanimously to keep its benchmark interest rate unchanged, citing persistent inflation that remains above its two-percent target.*

> *Fed Chair Jerome Powell told reporters that the central bank is not yet confident that inflation is on a sustained path back to target, signaling that rate cuts are still a ways off.*

> *Markets had hoped for a more dovish tone, but stocks slipped after Powell indicated policymakers would need to see several more months of favorable data before considering any easing of monetary policy.*

2. Because inflation remains above the Fed's two-percent target and is not yet on a sustained path back to that level.

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *a ways off* — 아직 멀었다 ("Rate cuts are still a ways off.")
- *dovish tone* — 완화적(비둘기파적) 태도 (금리 인하에 우호적인 입장)
- *favorable data* — 긍정적인 데이터, 좋은 경제 지표
- *borrowing costs* — 차입 비용 (대출 금리 등)
- *unanimous vote* — 만장일치 투표
