# 2026년 09월 14일 — Federal Reserve Holds Rates Steady Amid Inflation Concerns

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Keeps Benchmark Rate Unchanged as Policymakers Watch for Signs of Sustained Inflation Cooling"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The Fed kept its benchmark rate unchanged at 5.25 percent.* |
| **hold steady** | 유지하다, 변동 없다 | *Markets held steady after the Fed's announcement.* |
| **inflation cooling** | 인플레이션 완화 | *Signs of inflation cooling gave investors some relief.* |
| **policymaker** | 정책 입안자 | *Policymakers are watching jobs data closely this quarter.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *The central bank's monetary policy affects borrowing costs nationwide.* |
| **rate hike** | 금리 인상 | *Analysts no longer expect a rate hike before year-end.* |
| **yield curve** | 수익률 곡선 | *The yield curve briefly inverted, signaling recession fears.* |
| **soft landing** | 연착륙 (급격한 침체 없는 경기 조정) | *The Fed is aiming for a soft landing to tame inflation without triggering a recession.* |
| **consumer confidence** | 소비자 신뢰 지수 | *Consumer confidence rose slightly in August despite high prices.* |
| **tighten credit** | 신용 긴축하다 | *Banks began to tighten credit as default rates climbed.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The Federal Reserve voted unanimously today to hold its benchmark interest rate steady, signaling that officials want more evidence that inflation is on a sustained path downward before making any cuts."*

> *"Fed Chair Jerome Powell told reporters the central bank remains data-dependent, adding that a single positive inflation report is not enough to declare victory."*

> *"Wall Street responded with cautious optimism — the Dow Jones rose about one hundred twenty points following the announcement, while the ten-year Treasury yield ticked slightly lower."*
