# 2026년 10월 02일 — Congress Battles Over Spending Bill as Shutdown Looms

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Lawmakers race against the clock as a government shutdown threatens to halt federal operations, with both parties digging in over key spending provisions."**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **race against the clock** | 시간과 싸우다, 촉박하게 서두르다 | *Lawmakers are racing against the clock to pass a spending bill.* |
| **government shutdown** | 정부 셧다운 (연방 기관 운영 중단) | *A government shutdown would furlough thousands of federal workers.* |
| **spending bill** | 지출 법안 | *The spending bill allocates funds for defense and social programs.* |
| **dig in** | 버티다, 입장을 고수하다 | *Both parties dug in on their positions, refusing to compromise.* |
| **provision** | (법안·계약의) 조항, 규정 | *A key provision in the bill would cap discretionary spending.* |
| **bipartisan** | 초당적인, 양당 합의의 | *Passing the bill requires bipartisan support in the Senate.* |
| **stopgap measure** | 임시방편, 단기 해결책 | *Congress passed a stopgap measure to fund the government for 30 days.* |
| **fiscal year** | 회계연도 | *The new fiscal year begins on October 1st.* |
| **furlough** | (공무원 등을) 일시 해고하다 | *A shutdown could furlough up to 800,000 federal employees.* |
| **deadlock** | 교착 상태 | *Negotiations fell into a deadlock over defense spending levels.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The federal government is hours away from a potential shutdown after Congress failed to reach a deal on a new spending bill."*

> *"Both Republican and Democratic leaders are pointing fingers at each other, with little sign of a bipartisan breakthrough in sight."*

> *"A shutdown would suspend all non-essential federal services and delay paychecks for hundreds of thousands of government workers."*

## 이해도 확인

1. What event is Congress trying to avoid, and what is the deadline?
2. What does it mean when both parties are said to be "digging in"?
3. What are two consequences of a government shutdown mentioned in the broadcast?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. Congress is trying to avoid a government shutdown; the deadline is the start of the new fiscal year on October 1st.
2. "Digging in" means both parties are firmly holding their positions and refusing to compromise.
3. Two consequences are: suspension of non-essential federal services, and delayed paychecks for hundreds of thousands of government workers.

</details>

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *come to a standstill* — 완전히 멈추다 (The budget negotiations came to a standstill.)
- *hold the line* — 기존 입장을 유지하다 (Republicans are holding the line on tax cuts.)
- *reach across the aisle* — 상대 당과 협력하다 (She urged her colleagues to reach across the aisle.)
