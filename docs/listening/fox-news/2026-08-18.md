# 2026년 08월 18일 — Federal Reserve Holds Interest Rates Steady Amid Inflation Concerns

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Holds Rates Steady, Signals Caution as Inflation Remains Persistent Above Target"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **hold steady** | 현상 유지하다, 안정적으로 유지하다 | *The central bank decided to hold rates steady for the third consecutive meeting.* |
| **persistent** | 지속적인, 끈질긴 | *Persistent inflation has forced policymakers to remain cautious about rate cuts.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준금리 | *The Fed kept its benchmark rate unchanged at the current range.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *Tighter monetary policy is aimed at bringing inflation back to the 2% target.* |
| **signal caution** | 신중한 신호를 보내다 | *Fed officials signaled caution about cutting rates too soon.* |
| **data-dependent** | 데이터에 근거한, 데이터 의존적인 | *The committee described its approach as data-dependent going forward.* |
| **tighten the belt** | 허리띠를 졸라매다, 긴축하다 | *Consumers have started to tighten the belt as borrowing costs remain high.* |
| **rate-sensitive sector** | 금리에 민감한 부문 | *Housing is the most rate-sensitive sector, and it continues to feel the pressure.* |
| **labor market** | 노동 시장 | *A resilient labor market is giving the Fed room to maintain its current stance.* |
| **underlying inflation** | 근원 물가상승률 | *Underlying inflation, which strips out food and energy, came in at 3.1%.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

> *The Federal Reserve voted unanimously to hold its benchmark interest rate steady, citing persistent inflation and a resilient job market that gives policymakers little urgency to act.*

> *Fed Chair noted that while progress has been made, inflation remains above the two-percent target, and the committee is committed to staying the course until price stability is fully restored.*

> *Analysts on Wall Street reacted with mixed signals — stock futures dipped briefly before recovering, as investors parsed the Fed's statement for any hints about the timing of future rate cuts.*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *stay the course* — 끝까지 방침을 고수하다, 흔들리지 않고 나아가다
- *parse a statement* — 성명을 면밀히 분석하다, 한 줄 한 줄 따져 읽다
- *resilient economy* — 회복력 있는 경제, 탄탄한 경제
- *strip out (food and energy)* — (식품·에너지를) 제외하다 (근원 지표 산출 시 사용)
- *give little urgency* — 긴박하게 행동할 이유를 주지 않다
