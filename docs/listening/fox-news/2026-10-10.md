# 2026년 10월 10일 — Federal Reserve Holds Rates as Inflation Concerns Persist

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Keeps Interest Rates Unchanged, Signals Cautious Approach as Core Inflation Remains Stubbornly Elevated"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **hold steady** | 유지하다, 변동 없이 고수하다 | *The Fed decided to hold steady on interest rates for the third consecutive meeting.* |
| **core inflation** | 근원 인플레이션 (식품·에너지 제외) | *Core inflation remains above the Fed's 2% target.* |
| **stubbornly elevated** | 완고하게 높은 수준을 유지하는 | *Prices have remained stubbornly elevated despite monetary tightening.* |
| **monetary tightening** | 통화 긴축 | *Years of monetary tightening have slowed consumer spending.* |
| **yield curve** | 수익률 곡선 | *Investors are closely watching the yield curve for signs of a recession.* |
| **pivot** | 방향 전환 (정책 변화) | *Markets are pricing in a Fed pivot toward rate cuts next quarter.* |
| **dovish** | 완화적인, 비둘기파적인 | *The Fed chair's tone was more dovish than analysts had expected.* |
| **hawkish** | 긴축적인, 매파적인 | *Several committee members remain hawkish on inflation.* |
| **data-dependent** | 데이터에 기반한 | *The Fed reiterated its data-dependent approach to future decisions.* |
| **labor market tightness** | 노동시장 과열 | *Labor market tightness continues to put upward pressure on wages.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The Federal Reserve voted unanimously today to keep its benchmark interest rate unchanged at its current range, citing persistent inflationary pressures and a still-resilient labor market."*

> *"Fed Chair noted that while progress has been made on bringing inflation down, the central bank is not yet confident that inflation is on a sustained path back to its 2 percent target."*

> *"Wall Street responded with mixed results — the Dow edging higher, while bond yields climbed as traders reassessed the timeline for potential rate cuts heading into the fourth quarter."*

## 이해도 확인

1. Why did the Federal Reserve decide not to change interest rates at this meeting?
2. What is the Fed's official inflation target, and how does current inflation compare to it?
3. How did financial markets react to the Fed's announcement?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. The Fed kept rates unchanged due to persistent inflationary pressures and a still-resilient labor market, meaning conditions have not yet changed enough to justify a rate cut.
2. The Fed's target is 2% inflation. Current core inflation remains above that level, meaning the central bank has not yet achieved its goal.
3. Markets showed mixed reactions — the Dow edged higher, but bond yields climbed as traders pushed back their expectations for rate cuts.

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *price in* — (시장이 가능성을) 가격에 반영하다: *Markets have already priced in a rate cut for next year.*
- *put upward pressure on* — ~에 상승 압력을 가하다: *Higher oil prices put upward pressure on consumer costs.*
- *heading into* — ~을 앞두고, ~로 들어가면서: *Volatility is expected heading into the election season.*
