# 2026년 08월 24일 — Federal Reserve Signals Rate Cuts as Inflation Cools

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Chair Signals Imminent Rate Cuts as Inflation Data Shows Sustained Cooling"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **imminent** | 임박한, 곧 일어날 | *An imminent rate cut could boost consumer spending.* |
| **sustained** | 지속적인, 꾸준한 | *Sustained cooling in prices gave the Fed confidence to act.* |
| **pivot** | 방향 전환, 정책 전환 | *Markets are watching for a Fed pivot away from tight monetary policy.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *The central bank's monetary policy directly affects mortgage rates.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The Fed kept the benchmark rate unchanged for six consecutive meetings.* |
| **inflationary pressure** | 인플레이션 압력 | *Inflationary pressure has eased significantly over the past year.* |
| **dovish** | 비둘기파적인 (금리 인하 선호) | *The Fed Chair's dovish remarks sent stocks higher on Friday.* |
| **tighten / ease** | (금리를) 올리다 / 내리다 | *The Fed began to ease its grip on borrowing costs.* |
| **yield** | 수익률, 금리 | *Treasury yields fell sharply after the Fed's announcement.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell signaled today that the central bank is ready to cut interest rates, citing sustained progress on inflation and a moderating labor market."*

> *"The Fed's preferred inflation gauge, the Personal Consumption Expenditures index, fell to two-point-one percent year over year — just above the Fed's two-percent target."*

> *"Wall Street rallied sharply on the news, with the S&P 500 closing up nearly one-and-a-half percent, as investors priced in at least two rate cuts before year's end."*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *price in* — (시장이) 이미 반영하다 ("Markets have already priced in a September cut.")
- *year over year* — 전년 동기 대비 ("Inflation rose two percent year over year.")
- *moderating labor market* — 둔화되는 노동시장 ("A moderating labor market reduces wage-driven inflation.")
