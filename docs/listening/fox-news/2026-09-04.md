# 2026년 09월 04일 — Fed Holds Rates as Inflation Cools

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Federal Reserve Holds Interest Rates Steady as Inflation Falls to 2.1%, Economy Shows Resilience"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **hold steady** | 안정적으로 유지하다 | *The Fed decided to hold rates steady after months of debate.* |
| **inflation** | 인플레이션, 물가 상승 | *Inflation finally cooled to its lowest level in three years.* |
| **resilience** | 회복력, 탄력성 | *The job market showed remarkable resilience despite headwinds.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate remains at 4.25% for the third consecutive meeting.* |
| **consecutive** | 연속적인 | *This is the third consecutive month of declining consumer prices.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *Monetary policy decisions ripple through every sector of the economy.* |
| **pivot** | 방향 전환 | *Markets are betting on a rate pivot before year-end.* |
| **yield** | 수익률 | *Treasury yields dipped on the news of stable inflation.* |
| **labor market** | 노동 시장 | *A tight labor market continues to support consumer spending.* |
| **gross domestic product (GDP)** | 국내총생산 | *GDP grew by 2.4% in the second quarter, beating expectations.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The Federal Reserve voted unanimously today to hold its benchmark interest rate steady, citing progress on inflation while keeping a close eye on the labor market."*

> *"Fed Chair Jerome Powell told reporters that while inflation has retreated to near-target levels, policymakers want to see more data before committing to any rate cuts."*

> *"Wall Street responded positively to the announcement, with the Dow Jones rising more than two hundred points as investors interpreted the Fed's tone as cautiously optimistic."*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *hold the line* — 현재 입장을 고수하다 ("The Fed held the line on rates for the third straight meeting.")
- *data-dependent* — 데이터에 따라 결정하는 ("The committee remains data-dependent in its approach to future cuts.")
- *soft landing* — 경기 침체 없이 인플레이션을 낮추는 것 ("Economists praised the Fed's management of a rare soft landing.")
