# 2026년 08월 22일 — Federal Reserve Signals Rate Cut Ahead

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Chair Signals Potential Rate Cut as Inflation Cools, Labor Market Shows Resilience"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **signal** | 신호를 보내다, 암시하다 | *The Fed chair signaled a policy shift at the annual symposium.* |
| **rate cut** | 금리 인하 | *Markets rallied on expectations of a rate cut next quarter.* |
| **inflation** | 인플레이션, 물가 상승 | *Inflation has eased significantly over the past year.* |
| **resilience** | 회복력, 탄력성 | *The labor market has shown remarkable resilience despite headwinds.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *Monetary policy decisions affect borrowing costs for consumers and businesses.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate has been held at its current level for several months.* |
| **pivot** | 방향 전환 | *Investors are watching for any sign that the Fed is ready to pivot.* |
| **headwinds** | 역풍, 불리한 요인 | *The economy faces headwinds from weak global demand.* |
| **dovish** | 비둘기파적 (완화적) | *His remarks were interpreted as dovish by Wall Street analysts.* |
| **yield** | 수익률, 금리 | *Treasury yields fell sharply after the Fed statement was released.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell signaled Friday that the time has come to begin cutting interest rates, calling inflation's decline 'substantial' and warning that further cooling in the labor market would be unwelcome."*

> *"Wall Street reacted with broad gains, with the Dow Jones Industrial Average surging more than four hundred points as traders priced in a rate reduction at the Fed's September meeting."*

> *"Analysts say the shift marks a historic pivot after more than two years of aggressive rate hikes aimed at taming the worst inflation the United States had seen in four decades."*

3. Why had the Federal Reserve been raising interest rates over the past two years?

3. The Federal Reserve had been raising rates aggressively to combat the worst inflation the U.S. had seen in four decades.

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *price in* — (시장이) 미리 반영하다 ("Traders have already priced in a quarter-point cut.")
- *tame inflation* — 인플레이션을 잡다 ("The Fed's goal was to tame inflation without triggering a recession.")
- *labor market cooling* — 고용 시장 냉각 ("A cooling labor market gives the Fed room to ease policy.")
