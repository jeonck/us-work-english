# 2026년 09월 03일 — US Imposes New Tariffs on Chinese Tech Goods

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"White House Slaps Additional 25% Tariffs on Chinese Semiconductors and AI Hardware Amid Escalating Trade War"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **tariff** | 관세 | *The new tariff on semiconductors took effect immediately.* |
| **slap tariffs on** | ~에 관세를 부과하다 | *The administration slapped tariffs on imported steel and aluminum.* |
| **escalate** | 확대되다, 격화되다 | *Trade tensions between the two nations continued to escalate.* |
| **retaliation** | 보복 | *Beijing threatened swift retaliation if the measures were not reversed.* |
| **semiconductor** | 반도체 | *Semiconductors are critical components in modern electronics.* |
| **supply chain** | 공급망 | *Companies are diversifying their supply chains away from China.* |
| **levy** | 부과하다, 세금 | *Congress has the authority to levy taxes and duties.* |
| **countermeasures** | 대응 조치 | *China announced a series of countermeasures targeting US agricultural exports.* |
| **bilateral trade** | 양자 무역 | *Bilateral trade between the US and China exceeded $600 billion last year.* |
| **impose sanctions** | 제재를 가하다 | *The administration imposed sanctions on several Chinese tech firms.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The White House today announced sweeping new tariffs on Chinese-made semiconductors and artificial intelligence hardware, marking the most aggressive trade action against Beijing in years."*

> *"Commerce Secretary confirmed the measures will take effect within thirty days, giving American companies time to adjust their supply chains and source components from alternative partners."*

> *"China's Ministry of Commerce fired back, calling the tariffs, quote, 'a blatant act of economic coercion,' and warned of proportional countermeasures targeting US agricultural and energy exports."*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *trade deficit* — 무역 적자 (수입이 수출보다 많은 상태)
- *level the playing field* — 공평한 경쟁 환경을 만들다
- *tit-for-tat* — 맞대응, 보복 (상대방이 한 것과 똑같이 되갚는 것)
- *protectionist policy* — 보호무역주의 정책
- *free trade agreement* — 자유무역협정 (FTA)
