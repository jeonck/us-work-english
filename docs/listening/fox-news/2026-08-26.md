# 2026년 08월 26일 — Fed Signals Gradual Rate Cuts as Inflation Nears Target

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Federal Reserve Chair signals a cautious but steady path toward lower interest rates as core inflation inches closer to the central bank's 2 percent target."**

---

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell said Wednesday that the time has come to begin dialing back restrictive monetary policy, though he stopped short of committing to a specific timeline."*

> *"Core inflation, which excludes volatile food and energy prices, dropped to 2.3 percent last month — the lowest reading in more than three years — fueling hopes that the Fed's long battle against rising prices may finally be nearing its end."*

> *"Wall Street surged after the announcement, with the S&P 500 posting its biggest single-day gain in months, as traders priced in a higher probability of a quarter-point rate cut at the September meeting."*

## 이해도 확인

1. Why is the Federal Reserve considering cutting interest rates?
2. What does "core inflation" measure, and why is it important?
3. How did financial markets react to the Fed Chair's remarks?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. The Fed is considering rate cuts because inflation is cooling and moving closer to its 2% target, suggesting that the restrictive monetary policy has been effective.
2. Core inflation measures price increases excluding volatile food and energy costs. It gives a clearer picture of the underlying inflation trend and is used by the Fed to guide policy decisions.
3. Wall Street surged after the announcement — the S&P 500 posted its biggest single-day gain in months — as investors grew more confident that the Fed would cut rates at its next meeting.

</details>

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *dial back* — (강도를) 낮추다, 줄이다 ("The company dialed back its expansion plans.")
- *stop short of* — ~까지는 하지 않다, ~에 못 미치다 ("She stopped short of making a formal apology.")
- *price in* — (시장이 정보를) 이미 반영하다 ("Traders have already priced in a rate cut.")
