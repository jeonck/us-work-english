# 2026년 08월 17일 — Federal Reserve Holds Rates Amid Inflation Concerns

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Stands Pat as Inflation Proves Stickier Than Expected, Signals Fewer Cuts Ahead"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **stand pat** | 현상 유지하다, 그대로 두다 | *The Fed decided to stand pat on interest rates for the third consecutive meeting.* |
| **sticky inflation** | 잘 떨어지지 않는 인플레이션 | *Sticky inflation in the services sector is keeping policymakers on edge.* |
| **hawkish** | (금리에) 강경한, 긴축 선호의 | *Several hawkish Fed officials pushed back against early rate cuts.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate remained unchanged at its current level.* |
| **signal** | 시사하다, 신호를 보내다 | *The Fed signaled it would be data-dependent going forward.* |
| **data-dependent** | 데이터에 근거하여 결정하는 | *A data-dependent approach means every jobs report moves markets.* |
| **ease monetary policy** | 통화 완화 정책을 시행하다 | *Investors had hoped the Fed would ease monetary policy sooner.* |
| **yield** | (채권) 수익률 | *Treasury yields climbed after the Fed's announcement.* |
| **consensus** | 합의, 공감대 | *There was little consensus among Fed members about the path forward.* |
| **headwind** | 역풍, 불리한 요인 | *Persistent inflation remains a major headwind for the economy.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The Federal Reserve held interest rates steady today, defying calls from some economists who had predicted a cut heading into the fall."*

> *"Fed Chair Jerome Powell said the central bank remains committed to bringing inflation down to its two-percent target, warning that progress has been uneven."*

> *"Markets sold off sharply following the announcement, with investors pricing out hopes of a rate reduction before year's end."*

## 이해도 확인

1. What did the Federal Reserve decide at its latest meeting?
2. What reason did Fed Chair Powell give for the decision?
3. How did financial markets react to the Fed's announcement?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. The Federal Reserve decided to hold interest rates steady, making no changes to the benchmark rate.
2. Powell said the Fed remains committed to lowering inflation to its two-percent target and that progress has been uneven.
3. Markets sold off sharply, as investors had expected a rate cut and were disappointed by the decision.

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *higher for longer* — 금리를 오랫동안 높게 유지하는 정책 기조
- *price out* — (가능성을) 시장에서 반영하지 않다, 배제하다
- *on hold* — 보류 중인, 동결된 상태 (e.g., *rates are on hold*)
