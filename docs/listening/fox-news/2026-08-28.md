# 2026년 08월 28일 — Federal Reserve Holds Rates Amid Inflation Debate

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Stands Pat on Interest Rates, Signals Cautious Outlook as Inflation Remains Sticky"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **stand pat** | 현 상태를 유지하다 | *The Fed decided to stand pat on rates for the third consecutive meeting.* |
| **sticky inflation** | 좀처럼 내려가지 않는 인플레이션 | *Sticky inflation in the services sector is complicating the Fed's decision-making.* |
| **cautious outlook** | 신중한 전망 | *Officials issued a cautious outlook, citing global uncertainties.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *Monetary policy shifts can take months to ripple through the economy.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate has remained unchanged at 4.5% since March.* |
| **rein in** | 억제하다, 제어하다 | *The central bank is struggling to rein in consumer prices.* |
| **dovish / hawkish** | 완화적 / 긴축적 (정책 성향) | *A more dovish tone from the Fed sent stocks higher on Friday.* |
| **core PCE** | 근원 개인소비지출 물가지수 | *Core PCE rose 0.3% last month, above analysts' forecasts.* |
| **unanimous vote** | 만장일치 표결 | *The committee reached a unanimous vote to hold rates steady.* |
| **rate cut expectations** | 금리 인하 기대감 | *Markets dialed back rate cut expectations after the hawkish statement.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The Federal Reserve held interest rates steady today in a unanimous vote, signaling that policymakers are in no hurry to cut borrowing costs despite slowing economic growth."*

> *"Fed Chair Jerome Powell said the central bank needs more evidence that inflation is durably heading back to the two-percent target before easing monetary policy."*

> *"Wall Street responded with mixed signals — the Dow Jones slipped half a percent, while Treasury yields climbed as investors recalibrated their rate cut expectations for the remainder of the year."*

## 이해도 확인

1. What did the Federal Reserve decide at its latest meeting?
2. What condition did Fed Chair Powell say must be met before cutting rates?
3. How did financial markets react to the Fed's announcement?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. The Federal Reserve voted unanimously to hold interest rates steady, keeping the benchmark rate unchanged.
2. Powell said the Fed needs more evidence that inflation is durably moving back toward its 2% target before easing policy.
3. Markets reacted with mixed signals — the Dow Jones fell about 0.5%, and Treasury yields rose as investors reduced their expectations for rate cuts this year.
