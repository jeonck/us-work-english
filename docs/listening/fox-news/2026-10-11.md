# 2026년 10월 11일 — Federal Reserve Holds Rates Amid Inflation Uncertainty

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Keeps Rates Steady as Officials Weigh Stubborn Inflation Against Slowing Growth"**

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *The Federal Reserve voted unanimously on Wednesday to hold its benchmark interest rate steady, citing persistent inflationary pressures that have yet to fully retreat toward the central bank's two-percent target.*

> *Fed Chair Jerome Powell told reporters that while progress on inflation has been meaningful, the committee believes it is premature to declare victory, and that further evidence of sustained disinflation is needed before rate reductions can be considered.*

> *Stocks pulled back slightly on the news as investors had been hoping for a more definitive signal that rate cuts could come before the end of the year, with the S&P 500 closing down roughly half a percent on the session.*

## 이해도 확인

1. Why did the Federal Reserve choose to hold interest rates steady?
2. What did Fed Chair Powell say is needed before rate cuts can be considered?
3. How did the stock market react to the Fed's announcement?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. The Fed held rates steady because of persistent inflationary pressures that have not yet fully returned to the two-percent target.
2. Powell said further evidence of sustained disinflation (a continued decline in inflation) is needed before the committee will consider cutting rates.
3. Stocks pulled back slightly, with the S&P 500 closing down roughly half a percent, as investors had hoped for a clearer signal that rate cuts were coming before year-end.

</details>

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *tame inflation* — 인플레이션을 잡다 ("The government struggled to tame inflation.")
- *price in* — 이미 가격에 반영하다 ("Markets had already priced in a rate pause.")
- *pull back* — (주가가) 하락하다, 후퇴하다 ("The index pulled back after the announcement.")
- *reassess* — 재평가하다 ("The Fed said it would reassess the situation at its next meeting.")
- *benchmark rate* — 기준 금리 ("The benchmark rate has been unchanged for several months.")
