## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell suggested Tuesday that policymakers are on track to deliver one final quarter-point rate cut before the end of the year, provided inflation continues its downward trend."*

> *"Consumer prices rose just 2.1 percent year-over-year in August — the lowest reading in nearly three years — giving the Fed more room to ease monetary policy without risking a resurgence of price pressures."*

> *"Wall Street responded positively to the remarks, with the S&P 500 closing up more than one percent as investors priced in an 80 percent probability of a cut at the November meeting."*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *ease monetary policy* — 통화 정책을 완화하다 (금리를 내리거나 유동성을 공급하다)
- *price in* — (시장이 특정 가능성을) 미리 반영하다 ("Markets have already priced in a rate cut.")
- *year-over-year* — 전년 동기 대비 (경제 지표 비교 시 자주 쓰는 표현)
- *on track to* — ~할 궤도에 있다 ("The economy is on track to grow 2% this year.")
- *resurgence* — 재상승, 재등장 ("Officials want to avoid a resurgence of inflation.")
