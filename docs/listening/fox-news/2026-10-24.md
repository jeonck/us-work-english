# 2026년 10월 24일 — 중간선거 D-2주: 공화당 vs 민주당 의회 장악 경쟁

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"With midterms two weeks away, Republicans and Democrats make final push to control Congress as polls show razor-thin margins in key battleground states."**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **midterm elections** | 중간선거 | *The midterm elections are held two years into a presidential term.* |
| **razor-thin margin** | 아주 근소한 차이 | *Polls show a razor-thin margin between the two candidates.* |
| **battleground state** | 경합주 | *Pennsylvania is considered a key battleground state this cycle.* |
| **make a final push** | 마지막 총력전을 펼치다 | *Both parties are making a final push to energize their base.* |
| **congressional majority** | 의회 다수당 | *Winning the congressional majority would give them legislative power.* |
| **turnout** | 투표율, 투표 참여 | *High voter turnout often favors Democratic candidates.* |
| **early voting** | 사전 투표 | *Millions have already cast ballots through early voting.* |
| **incumbents** | 현직자들 | *Several incumbents are facing tough reelection challenges.* |
| **canvass** | (지지자 확보를 위해) 유세하다 | *Volunteers have been canvassing neighborhoods for weeks.* |
| **down-ballot races** | 하위 선거 (주지사, 시의원 등) | *Down-ballot races will also shape the political landscape.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"With less than two weeks until Election Day, both parties are making their final arguments to voters. Republicans are zeroing in on inflation and border security, while Democrats are hammering the message of protecting democracy and reproductive rights."*

> *"New polling out of three key battleground states shows the races are essentially tied, meaning turnout will be the decisive factor on November 3rd. Campaign operatives on both sides are pouring resources into get-out-the-vote efforts."*

> *"The stakes couldn't be higher. Control of the Senate and the House will determine whether the White House can advance its legislative agenda — or face two years of gridlock heading into the 2028 presidential race."*

## 이해도 확인

1. What two main issues are Republicans focusing on in this election?
2. What does "razor-thin margin" mean, and why does it matter in this context?
3. According to the broadcast, what will be the "decisive factor" on Election Day?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. Republicans are focusing on inflation and border security.
2. "Razor-thin margin" means an extremely narrow difference between candidates. It matters because it means either side could win, making every vote count.
3. Voter turnout will be the decisive factor, as both candidates are essentially tied in polls.

</details>

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *zero in on* — ~에 집중하다, 초점을 맞추다 ("Republicans are zeroing in on the economy.")
- *pour resources into* — ~에 자원을 쏟아붓다 ("Both campaigns are pouring resources into swing states.")
- *get-out-the-vote effort* — 투표 독려 운동 ("Volunteers are leading get-out-the-vote efforts nationwide.")
- *legislative agenda* — 입법 의제 ("The president's legislative agenda stalled in the Senate.")
- *face gridlock* — 교착 상태에 빠지다 ("Without a majority, they may face two years of gridlock.")
