# 2026년 09월 23일 — Federal Reserve Holds Rates Amid Inflation Concerns

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Keeps Benchmark Rate Unchanged as Policymakers Monitor Persistent Inflation and Labor Market Data"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The Fed left the benchmark rate at its current level for the third straight meeting.* |
| **policymakers** | 정책 입안자들 | *Policymakers are carefully weighing the risks of cutting too early.* |
| **persistent inflation** | 지속적인 인플레이션 | *Persistent inflation in the services sector is complicating the Fed's decision.* |
| **hawkish stance** | 매파적 입장 (금리 인상 선호) | *Several board members maintained a hawkish stance at Tuesday's meeting.* |
| **labor market tightness** | 노동 시장 과열 | *Labor market tightness continues to put upward pressure on wages.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *The Fed's monetary policy affects mortgage rates and business borrowing.* |
| **cooling off** | 냉각되다, 안정되다 | *Consumer spending appears to be cooling off after the summer surge.* |
| **soft landing** | 연착륙 (경기 침체 없이 인플레 억제) | *Economists are debating whether the Fed can pull off a soft landing.* |
| **yield curve** | 수익률 곡선 | *Investors are watching the yield curve for signs of economic slowdown.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The Federal Reserve held its benchmark interest rate steady today, signaling that officials are in no rush to cut borrowing costs despite slowing economic growth."*

> *"Fed Chair Jerome Powell told reporters that while inflation has moved closer to the central bank's two-percent target, policymakers need to see more sustained progress before easing monetary policy."*

> *"Markets had been pricing in a rate cut as early as next month, but today's statement suggests the Fed is adopting a more cautious, data-driven approach heading into the fourth quarter."*

## 이해도 확인

1. Why did the Federal Reserve decide to hold interest rates steady?
2. What condition must be met before the Fed considers cutting rates?
3. How had markets reacted prior to today's announcement?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. The Fed held rates steady because officials want to see more sustained progress on inflation before easing monetary policy, despite slowing economic growth.
2. Policymakers need to see inflation move closer to — and remain near — the two-percent target before cutting rates.
3. Markets had been pricing in (expecting) a rate cut as early as next month, suggesting investors were more optimistic about an imminent cut than the Fed turned out to be.

</details>
