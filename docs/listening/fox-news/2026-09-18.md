# 2026년 09월 18일 — Federal Reserve Signals Rate Cut as Inflation Cools

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Chair Signals Imminent Rate Cut as Core Inflation Falls to Two-Year Low, Boosting Market Confidence"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **imminent** | 임박한, 곧 닥칠 | *An imminent rate cut sent stocks surging on Wall Street.* |
| **core inflation** | 근원 물가 상승률 (식품·에너지 제외) | *Core inflation dropped to 2.1%, its lowest level in two years.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *The Fed's monetary policy aims to balance growth and price stability.* |
| **pivot** | 방향 전환, 노선 변경 | *Investors are betting on a Fed pivot toward looser policy.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate has held at 5.25% for six consecutive months.* |
| **cooling** | 냉각되고 있는, 둔화되는 | *A cooling labor market supports the case for easing rates.* |
| **easing cycle** | 완화 사이클 | *Analysts expect the easing cycle to last through mid-2027.* |
| **yield** | 수익률, 금리 | *Treasury yields fell sharply after the Fed's announcement.* |
| **signal** | (말·행동으로) 암시하다, 신호를 보내다 | *The chair signaled a 25-basis-point cut at the next meeting.* |
| **consensus** | 합의, 컨센서스 | *There is growing consensus inside the Fed for a September cut.* |

---

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell signaled today that policymakers are growing more confident that inflation is sustainably returning to the two-percent target."*

> *"Core inflation came in at 2.1 percent last month — the lowest reading in more than two years — giving the Fed the green light to begin cutting its benchmark interest rate as early as next month."*

> *"Markets reacted swiftly, with the S&P 500 closing up more than one and a half percent as investors priced in a quarter-point rate reduction at the Fed's October meeting."*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *give the green light* — 허가하다, 승인하다 ("The data gave the Fed the green light to cut rates.")
- *price in* — (시장이) 예상치를 이미 반영하다 ("Markets have already priced in a 25-basis-point cut.")
- *as early as* — 이르면 ~에 ("The cut could happen as early as next month.")
- *hold steady* — 변동 없이 유지되다 ("The benchmark rate has held steady since March.")
- *on the horizon* — 곧 다가오고 있는 ("Further cuts are on the horizon if data cooperates.")
