# 2026년 09월 10일 — Federal Reserve Cuts Interest Rates Amid Economic Slowdown

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Slashes Rates for Third Time This Year as Inflation Cools and Growth Weakens"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **slash rates** | 금리를 대폭 인하하다 | *The Fed slashed rates by a quarter point to stimulate borrowing.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *The committee debated whether current monetary policy was too restrictive.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The Federal Reserve cut its benchmark interest rate by a quarter of a percentage point today, marking the third reduction this year as policymakers cited easing inflation and a cooling labor market."*

> *"Fed Chair Jerome Powell told reporters the central bank remains data-dependent, and that further adjustments will hinge on whether inflation continues its downward trajectory toward the two-percent target."*

> *"Markets rallied on the news, with the Dow Jones jumping more than three hundred points, while mortgage rates are expected to drift lower in the coming weeks, offering some relief to prospective homebuyers."*

2. What two economic conditions gave the Fed reason to lower rates?
3. What effect did the rate cut announcement have on the stock market?

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *hinge on* — ~에 달려 있다 ("The outcome hinges on next month's jobs report.")
- *data-dependent* — 데이터에 따라 결정하는 ("Our strategy is data-dependent, not fixed.")
- *drift lower* — 서서히 하락하다 ("Borrowing costs are expected to drift lower over the quarter.")
- *tighten / ease policy* — 정책을 긴축하다 / 완화하다 ("The Fed decided to ease policy given weakening growth.")
- *prospective* — 잠재적인, 예비의 ("Prospective buyers are watching mortgage rates closely.")
