# 2026년 09월 06일 — Fed Rate Decision Sparks Economic Debate

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Federal Reserve Holds Rates Steady Amid Inflation Concerns, Signals Caution Heading Into Election Season"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **hold rates steady** | 금리를 동결하다 | *The Fed voted to hold rates steady for the third consecutive meeting.* |
| **inflation concerns** | 인플레이션 우려 | *Inflation concerns continue to weigh on consumer confidence.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *The committee's monetary policy stance remains restrictive.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate has remained above five percent all year.* |
| **rein in** | 억제하다, 통제하다 | *Officials are determined to rein in stubborn price pressures.* |
| **rate cut** | 금리 인하 | *Markets had priced in a rate cut by the end of Q3.* |
| **consumer sentiment** | 소비자 심리 | *Consumer sentiment slipped again as borrowing costs remain elevated.* |
| **signal caution** | 신중한 자세를 취하다 | *The Fed chairman signaled caution about moving too fast on cuts.* |
| **economic headwinds** | 경제적 역풍 | *Global economic headwinds are adding pressure to domestic growth.* |
| **pivot** | (정책) 전환 | *Investors are watching for any signal of a policy pivot.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The Federal Reserve voted unanimously today to hold its benchmark interest rate steady, citing persistent inflation pressures that have made policymakers cautious about easing too soon."*

> *"Fed Chair Jerome Powell said the committee needs more evidence that inflation is on a sustained path back to its two-percent target before it can justify cutting borrowing costs."*

> *"With midterm elections just weeks away, the decision is drawing sharp criticism from both political parties — conservatives calling the Fed too slow, while others warn that any premature cut could reignite price pressures."*

## 이해도 확인

1. Why did the Federal Reserve decide to hold interest rates steady?
2. What condition does the Fed say must be met before it will cut rates?
3. How are politicians reacting to the Fed's decision?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. Because of persistent inflation pressures that made policymakers cautious about easing monetary policy too soon.
2. The Fed wants more evidence that inflation is on a sustained path back to its two-percent target.
3. Both parties are critical — conservatives say the Fed is moving too slowly, while others warn that a premature rate cut could reignite inflation.

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *price pressures* — 물가 상승 압력
- *tighten monetary policy* — 통화 정책을 긴축하다
- *soft landing* — 연착륙 (경기침체 없이 인플레이션을 잡는 것)
- *dovish / hawkish* — 비둘기파(완화적) / 매파(긴축적)
- *above expectations* — 예상치를 웃도는
- *data-dependent* — 데이터에 의존하는 (경제 지표에 따라 결정)
