# 2026년 10월 17일 — US Economy: Federal Reserve Holds Interest Rates Steady

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Federal Reserve Holds Rates Steady, Signals Cautious Approach Amid Inflation Uncertainty"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **hold steady** | 유지하다, 변동 없이 유지하다 | *The Fed decided to hold rates steady for another quarter.* |
| **inflation** | 인플레이션, 물가 상승 | *Persistent inflation has made it difficult for families to make ends meet.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *The Federal Reserve's monetary policy affects borrowing costs nationwide.* |
| **interest rate** | 금리, 이자율 | *A rise in interest rates means higher mortgage payments for homeowners.* |
| **cautious approach** | 신중한 접근 방식 | *The central bank is taking a cautious approach before making any rate cuts.* |
| **benchmark rate** | 기준 금리 | *The benchmark rate has remained unchanged for several consecutive meetings.* |
| **economic outlook** | 경제 전망 | *The Fed's economic outlook remains uncertain due to global headwinds.* |
| **labor market** | 노동 시장 | *Despite strong labor market data, officials remain concerned about price stability.* |
| **yield** | 수익률, 이자 수익 | *Bond yields rose slightly following the Fed's announcement.* |
| **pivot** | (정책) 전환 | *Investors are anticipating a potential policy pivot by early next year.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The Federal Reserve voted unanimously today to hold its benchmark interest rate steady, citing persistent inflation concerns and a resilient labor market."*

> *"Fed Chair Jerome Powell said policymakers are prepared to keep rates elevated for as long as necessary to bring inflation down to the two-percent target."*

> *"Wall Street reacted with mixed signals, as investors had hoped for clearer guidance on when the central bank might begin cutting rates."*
