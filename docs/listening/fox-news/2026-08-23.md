# 2026년 08월 23일 — U.S. Job Market Cools as Fed Weighs Rate Cut

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Jobs Report Comes In Below Expectations, Raising Pressure on Fed to Cut Rates"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **nonfarm payrolls** | 비농업 부문 고용자 수 | *Nonfarm payrolls rose by only 140,000 last month.* |
| **below expectations** | 예상치를 밑도는 | *The report came in below expectations for the third straight month.* |
| **weigh a rate cut** | 금리 인하를 검토하다 | *The Fed is weighing a rate cut at its September meeting.* |
| **cooling labor market** | 냉각되는 노동 시장 | *A cooling labor market could ease inflationary pressure.* |
| **unemployment rate** | 실업률 | *The unemployment rate ticked up to 4.3 percent.* |
| **hiring freeze** | 채용 동결 | *Several tech firms have implemented a hiring freeze.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *The Fed's monetary policy will be closely watched this fall.* |
| **pivot** | (정책) 방향 전환 | *Analysts are betting on a policy pivot toward lower rates.* |
| **yield** | 수익률, 금리 | *Treasury yields fell sharply after the jobs data was released.* |
| **recession fears** | 경기 침체 우려 | *Recession fears are mounting on Wall Street.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The latest jobs report is raising fresh concerns about the health of the American economy, with nonfarm payrolls falling well below Wall Street's forecasts."*

> *"The Federal Reserve now faces growing pressure to cut interest rates, as a cooling labor market signals that its aggressive tightening campaign may have gone too far."*

> *"Treasury yields dropped sharply on the news, while stocks rallied on hopes that cheaper borrowing costs could be on the way."*
