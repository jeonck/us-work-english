# 2026년 08월 10일 — Federal Reserve Holds Rates Amid Inflation Concerns

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Stands Pat on Interest Rates as Officials Warn Inflation Fight Is 'Not Over Yet'"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **stand pat** | 현 상태를 유지하다 | *The Fed chose to stand pat on rates for the third consecutive meeting.* |
| **monetary policy** | 통화 정책 | *Monetary policy decisions affect borrowing costs across the economy.* |
| **hawkish** | (금리 인상에) 강경한 입장의 | *Several hawkish officials pushed for another rate hike.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *"The Federal Reserve held interest rates steady today, as policymakers signaled they need more evidence that inflation is sustainably returning to their two-percent target."*

> *"Fed Chair Jerome Powell told reporters that while progress has been made, officials are not yet confident enough to begin cutting borrowing costs, warning that the battle against inflation is, quote, 'not over yet.'"*

> *"Markets had been expecting a rate cut by year's end, but today's more cautious tone from the Fed sent stocks lower and pushed Treasury yields higher across the board."*

## 이해도 확인

1. What did the Federal Reserve decide to do with interest rates at this meeting?
2. What condition did Fed officials say must be met before they consider cutting rates?
3. How did financial markets react to the Fed's announcement?

<details>
<summary>정답 보기</summary>

1. The Federal Reserve decided to hold (keep) interest rates steady — they did not raise or cut them.
2. Officials said they need more evidence that inflation is sustainably returning to their two-percent target before cutting rates.
3. Markets reacted negatively — stocks fell and Treasury yields rose in response to the cautious tone.

</details>

- *rate cut / rate hike* — 금리 인하 / 금리 인상 ("Investors are pricing in at least two rate cuts before the end of the year.")
