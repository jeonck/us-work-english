# 2026년 10월 01일 — Federal Reserve Holds Rates Amid Inflation Debate

## 오늘의 헤드라인

> **"Fed Holds Interest Rates Steady as Policymakers Weigh Inflation Risk Against Economic Slowdown"**

---

## 핵심 단어 & 표현

| 단어 / 표현 | 뜻 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| **hold steady** | 현 수준을 유지하다 | *The Fed decided to hold rates steady this quarter.* |
| **policymaker** | 정책 입안자 | *Policymakers are divided on the next move.* |
| **inflation** | 인플레이션, 물가 상승 | *Inflation remains a top concern for American households.* |
| **economic slowdown** | 경기 둔화 | *Signs of an economic slowdown prompted caution.* |
| **hawkish** | (금리 인상을 선호하는) 매파적 | *Several hawkish members pushed for another rate hike.* |
| **dovish** | (금리 인하를 선호하는) 비둘기파적 | *Dovish officials warned against overtightening.* |
| **interest rate** | 금리, 이자율 | *Higher interest rates make borrowing more expensive.* |
| **Federal Reserve (Fed)** | 미국 연방준비제도 | *The Federal Reserve meets eight times a year.* |
| **gross domestic product (GDP)** | 국내총생산 | *GDP growth slowed to 1.8% in the second quarter.* |
| **tighten monetary policy** | 통화 긴축 정책을 시행하다 | *The central bank may tighten monetary policy further.* |

## 방송 발췌 (섀도잉용)

앵커 목소리처럼 또렷하고 자신 있게 읽어본다.

> *The Federal Reserve voted unanimously to hold interest rates steady at its latest policy meeting, signaling a cautious approach as inflation shows mixed signals.*

> *Fed Chair Jerome Powell said policymakers remain data-dependent, adding that any future rate adjustments will hinge on incoming economic reports, particularly on jobs and consumer prices.*

> *Critics on Capitol Hill argue the Fed has been too slow to cut rates, warning that prolonged high borrowing costs could tip the economy into recession heading into the holiday season.*

## 관련 표현 더 보기

- *data-dependent* — 데이터에 근거하여 결정하는 (경제 지표에 따라 정책을 결정하는 방식)
- *hinge on* — ~에 달려 있다 (예: *The outcome hinges on next month's job numbers.*)
- *tip into recession* — 경기침체로 빠져들다 (예: *Rising debt could tip the economy into recession.*)
